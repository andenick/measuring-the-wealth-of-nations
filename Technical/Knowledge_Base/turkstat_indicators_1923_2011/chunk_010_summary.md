## Contents

### Table 11.42 (continued) — Employment and Basic Indicators in Financial Intermediary Institutions by Economic Activity

All sub-tables in this chunk continue from chunk_009 for the same ISIC Rev.3 sector breakdown, plus a new NACE Rev.2 series starting from 2002.

#### ISIC Rev.3 series (1993–2001) — final sub-tables:

1. **Input (Girdi) — Million TL** (pp. 359–360):
   - 1993–1997 and 1998–2001 panels
   - Total financial sector input surges from 107bn TL (1993) to 82.9 trillion TL (2001)
   - Banking sector accounts for ~80–90% of total input

2. **Output (Çıktı) — Million TL** (pp. 361–362):
   - 1993–1997 and 1998–2001 panels
   - Total output grows from 202bn TL (1993) to 97.4 trillion TL (2001)
   - Dramatic nominal growth reflects hyperinflation throughout period

3. **Value Added (Katma Değer) — Million TL** (pp. 363–364):
   - 1993–1997 and 1998–2001 panels
   - Total value added: 96bn TL (1993) → 14.5 trillion TL (2001)
   - Note: Factoring sector (6599) shows negative value added in 2001 (-188bn TL), likely 2001 banking crisis effect

#### NACE Rev.2 series (2002–2010) — new classification:

4. **Number of Enterprises (Girişim sayısı)** (pp. 365–366):
   - 2002–2008 and 2009–2010 panels
   - Total financial sector enterprises nearly triple 2002→2010 (5,158 → 17,945)
   - Growth concentrated in insurance agents (66.22): 4,017 → 15,014
   - New subcategories added over time (66.11 stock markets, 66.21 risk/damage evaluation, 66.30 fund management)

5. **Annual Average Number of Persons Engaged (Çalışanların yıllık ortalama sayısı)** (pp. 367–368):
   - 2002–2008 and 2009–2010 panels
   - Total financial sector employment: 185,540 (2002) → 294,603 (2010)
   - Bank-related employment (64.11/64.19): 131,127 (2002) → 187,824 (2010)
   - Insurance agent employment (66.22) surges: 15,257 (2002) → 56,289 (2010)

## Relevance to ST2 / National Accounts Focus
- **Low direct relevance** for economy-wide GDP, GSYH, or aggregate compensation of employees
- Value added (katma değer) data is present but only for the financial intermediaries sector, not the full economy
- The gross payments to employees tables (chunk_009 table 08) and the value added tables here are sector-specific financial sector data only
- No milli gelir, no economy-wide ücret/istihdam aggregate tables in this chunk
- The NACE Rev.2 persons-engaged series (2002–2010) is the most complete employment series available in these two chunks for any sector

## Key Observations Across Chunks 009–010
- These chunks cover industrial output indices, specific manufactured goods production, and a detailed multi-dimensional dataset on the Turkish financial intermediary sector (1993–2010)
- The financial intermediaries dataset (Table 11.42) is comprehensive but sector-specific — it cannot substitute for economy-wide national accounts aggregates
- For ST2 compensation-of-employees and labor share series, the relevant data must lie in earlier or later chunks (likely Chapter 12 National Accounts or dedicated labor/wage chapters)

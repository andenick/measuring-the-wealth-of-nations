## Tables extracted

## Methodological pages (no numerical tables)
- **pp.246–247**: Table of contents for Section 11 (Industry and Business Statistics). Lists 44 sub-tables covering manufacturing, production indices, capacity utilisation, wages index, employment index.
- **pp.248–250**: Explanation of Annual Manufacturing Industry Statistics — census history from 1915, coverage criteria (10+ employees), definitions of output/input/value added/wages.
  - Key definition: "Annual payments to employees" (ücretle çalışanlara yapılan yıllık ödemeler) = gross wages including overtime, bonuses, payments in kind; EXCLUDES employer social security contributions.

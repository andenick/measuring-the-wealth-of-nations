## Content Overview

### Chapter 1 Tail: Population / Nüfus (Tables 1.21–1.26)
Literacy rates by sex (census years 1935–2000 and ABPRS 2008–2011), employed population by economic activity and sex (census years 1955–2000), labour force participation rates.

### Chapter 7 Tail: Elections / Seçim (Table 7.7, Page 127)
Results of metropolitan municipality mayoral elections 1984–2009. Votes by political party. Not relevant to national accounts.

### Chapter 8: Labour / Çalışma (Pages 128–129)
Chapter table of contents (Tables 8.1–8.19) and methodological explanation of the Household Labour Force Survey (HİA/HLFS). Lists survey history from 1966 to present; describes transition from semi-annual (1988–1999) to monthly (2000+) surveys, ILO methodology, EU/Eurostat harmonisation from 2004.

## Content Overview

This chunk contains the continuation of Chapter 19 (Public Finance), covering consolidated and central government budget balance tables for 1980–2011. The key data of labor share relevance is the **Personnel Giderleri** (Compensation of Employees / Personel harcamaları) series embedded within the expenditure breakdown — this is the closest available proxy for "ücret" (wages/compensation) in the public sector.

## Tables

### Table 01: Consolidated Budget Revenues (Old Series) — 1980–2003
`chunk_017_table01_consolidated_budget_revenues_old_series.csv`
- Annual revenue totals, tax revenues (direct + indirect), nontax revenues, annexed budget, grants
- Revenues/GNP (%) column: ranges 12.7% (1984) to 22.0% (2003)
- Unit: Milyar TL (Billion TL), at current prices, cumulative

### Table 02: Consolidated Budget Expenditures (Old Series) — 1980–2003
`chunk_017_table02_consolidated_budget_expenditures_old_series.csv`
- **KEY SERIES**: Personnel giderleri (Personel) = compensation of employees in central government
  - 1980: 342 Bn TL → 2003: 30,200,762 Bn TL (nominal growth reflects hyperinflation)
- Columns: Total expenditures, current expenditures, personnel, other current, investment, transfers
- Unit: Milyar TL (Billion TL), at current prices

### Table 03: Consolidated Budget Interest Payments & Balance (Old Series) — 1980–2003
`chunk_017_table03_consolidated_budget_interest_and_balance_old_series.csv`
- Interest payments (total, foreign, domestic), transfers to SEEs, other transfers
- Budget balance and budget balance as % of GNP/GDP
- Expenditures/GNP (%): 15.1% (1982) to 33.2% (2001)
- Cash balance series also included
- Notable: 2001 crisis year — budget deficit peaked at -12.0% of GDP

### Table 04: Central Government Budget Balance (New Series) — 2004–2011
`chunk_017_table04_consolidated_budget_revenues_expenditures_2004_2011.csv`
- **KEY SERIES**: Personnel Giderleri = Compensation of Employees
  - 2004: 28,947,995 (Bn TL) → 2006: 37,812 → 2011: 72,914 (Mn TL, after redenomination)
- Also: Social Security Contributions (SGK Devlet Primi), Goods & Services, Interest, Transfers
- Primary balance, budget balance, cash balance
- Unit changes: 2004–2005 Bn TL; 2006–2011 Mn TL (post-redenomination)

### Table 05: Percentage Breakdown of Consolidated Budget Revenues (Old Series) — 1980–2003
`chunk_017_table05_consolidated_budget_revenue_pct_breakdown_old_series.csv`
- Revenue shares: direct taxes shifted from 57% (1982) to 27.7% (2003); indirect taxes rose to 56.4% (2003)

## Relevance to ST2 / Labor Share

- **Personnel giderleri** (Tables 02, 04) = public sector compensation of employees. This is a component of national income flows relevant to labor share analysis.
- **No direct GSYH/GDP series** in this chunk — GDP denominators for labor share calculation must come from other chapters.
- Personnel as % of total expenditures declined over the period as interest payments ballooned (1990s fiscal crisis).
- The 2006–2011 data uses new TL (post-6-zero redenomination of 2005).

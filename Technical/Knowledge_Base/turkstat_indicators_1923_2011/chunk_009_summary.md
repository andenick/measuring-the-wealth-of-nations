# Chunk 009 Summary — TurkStat Statistical Indicators 1923-2011

**Pages covered**: Approx. pp. 338–358 (book pagination)
**Chapter**: Chapter 11 — Industry / Labour / Financial Intermediaries

## Contents

### Table 11.39 — Quarterly Industrial Hours Worked Index (2005=100)
- Coverage: 2005–2011
- Three sub-tables:
  1. **Main industrial groupings**: Total industry, Intermediate goods, Durable consumer goods, Non-durable consumer goods, Energy, Capital goods
  2. **Mining and quarrying**: Total, Coal/lignite, Crude petroleum/natural gas, Metal ores, Other mining
  3. **Manufacturing industry (partial, continued from prior chunk)**: Manufacturing total, Food, Beverages, Tobacco, Textiles, Wearing apparel
- Notable: Sharp dip across all categories in 2009 (GFC impact); recovery in 2010–2011

### Table 11.41 — Production of Selected Industrial Products (enterprises 20+ employees, continued)
- Coverage: 2005–2010
- Products: Coaxial cables (kg), Other electric conductors (kg), Refrigerators (pieces), Washing/drying machines, Fans/hoods, Vacuum cleaners, Ovens, Tractors, Automobiles, Buses/minibuses, Trucks, Tire rims (kg), Bicycles
- Notable: Automobile production peaked at ~900k units in 2008; sharp truck/bus drops in 2009

### Table 11.42 — Employment and Basic Indicators in Financial Intermediary Institutions by Economic Activity (ISIC Rev.3, 1993–2001)
- Coverage: 1993–2001 across multiple sub-tables
- Sub-tables extracted:
  1. **Number of enterprises/establishments** (Teşebbüs/işyeri sayısı): 1993–1997, 1998–2001
  2. **Annual average number of employees** (Ücretle çalışanların yıllık ortalama sayısı): 1993–1997, 1998–2001
  3. **Annual average number of persons engaged** (Çalışanların yıllık ortalama sayısı): 1993–1997, 1998–2001
  4. **Annual gross payments to employees** (Ücretle çalışanlara yapılan yıllık brüt ödemeler, Million TL): 1993–1997, 1998–2001
- Sectors: Banks (incl. Central Bank), Financial Leasing, Agricultural Credit Cooperatives, Factoring, Life Insurance, Non-life Insurance, Mutual Funds, Foreign Exchange Offices, Insurance Agency
- Notable: Banking sector dominates employment (~90%); severe employment contraction post-2001 crisis (banks shed ~50k workers 2000→2001)

## Relevance to ST2 / National Accounts Focus
- **Low direct relevance** for GDP/compensation of employees time series
- Table 11.42 gross payments to employees is sector-specific (financial intermediaries only), NOT economy-wide compensation of employees
- No GSYH, milli gelir, or economy-wide ücret tables in this chunk

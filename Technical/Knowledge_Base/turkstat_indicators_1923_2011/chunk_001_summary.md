# chunk_001 Summary — TurkStat Statistical Indicators 1923-2011

## Document Range
Pages III–27 of the full publication (front matter through Chapter 1: Population / Nüfus).

## Content Overview

### Front Matter (Pages III–VI)
- **Foreword (Önsöz / Foreword)**: Statement from TurkStat President Birol Aydemir on the purpose and history of Statistical Indicators 1923-2011, first published August 1991.
- **Table of Contents (İçindekiler / Contents)**: Lists all 20 chapters of the publication. Key chapter of interest: **Chapter 20 — Ulusal Hesaplar / National Accounts** (starting page 688).
- **Explanation (Açıklama)**: Sources of data, notes on rounding, and currency notes (YTL→TL from 01.01.2009).

### Chapter 1: Population / Nüfus (Pages 1–27)
Contains population data from 1927–2011 census results and Address Based Population Registration System (ADNKS/ABPRS) data from 2007–2011.

## Key Data Points
- GDP (1987 prices) grew from 50.3 trillion TL (1980) to 156.2 trillion TL (2006)
- Private consumption share peaked at ~80.5% (1982, current prices) and trended to ~69% by 2006
- Public GFCF share of total GFCF fell from ~53% (1980) to ~17% (1995 trough, current prices)
- Real GDP growth rate: 1994 crisis shows -5.0%; 2001 crisis shows -7.5%; 2004 recovery: +9.0%

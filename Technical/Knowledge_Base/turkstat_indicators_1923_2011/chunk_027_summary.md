### Page 769 — Table 20.36: Growth Rates of GDP by Cost Components, Current Prices (1981–2006)

**Title (TR)**: Gelir yöntemiyle Gayri Safi Yurtiçi Hasıla büyüme hızı
**Title (EN)**: Growth rates of Gross Domestic Product by cost components (At current prices)
**Unit**: Percent (%)
**Coverage**: 1981–2006 (26 annual observations, year-on-year nominal growth)

**Key observations**:
- Nominal growth rates reflect Turkey's high-inflation era; GDP growth exceeded 100% in 1994–1995 and 1996
- Subsidies growth was highly volatile: -15.1% (1987), +160.1% (1996), -46.9% (2002), reflecting policy shifts
- Operating surplus growth tracked closely with overall GDP growth; compensation of employees growth diverged notably in crisis years (1994: 61.4% vs GDP 95.2%)
- Post-2001 disinflation visible: GDP growth fell from 43.2% (2001) to 13.2% (2005) at current prices

### Page 770 — Table 20.37: GDP Share by Cost Components, Current Prices (1980–2006)

**Title (TR)**: Gelir yöntemiyle Gayri Safi Yurtiçi Hasılanın yüzde dağılımı
**Title (EN)**: Share of Gross Domestic Product by cost components (At current prices)
**Unit**: Percent (%) of GDP
**Coverage**: 1980–2006 (27 annual observations)

**Key observations**:
- **Compensation of employees** (labor share): 27.1% in 1980, declined through 1980s to ~19–20%, rose again through 1990s crisis to 31.9% in 1991, then trended down to 26.2% by 2006. This is the critical wage share / functional income distribution series.
- **Operating surplus** (profit share): inverse of labor — 62.8% (1980), peaked 67.7% (1985), fell during early 1990s expansion to 49.4% (2001 crisis), recovered to ~50% by 2006
- **Net taxes on production**: secular upward trend from 5.2% (1980) to 17.5% (2006), reflecting broader tax base and VAT expansion
- **Consumption of fixed capital**: relatively stable 5–8% of GDP across period
- **Subsidies**: declined from 2.1% (1980) to near-zero 0.2% (2004–2006), reflecting structural reform

## ST2 / NickyData Relevance

These three tables provide the income-side decomposition of Turkish GDP:

- **Table 20.35** (levels): Use for nominal GDP income components — compensation of employees (wage bill), operating surplus (profit), consumption of fixed capital (CCA), net taxes
- **Table 20.37** (shares): **Primary interest for ST2** — functional income distribution (labor share, profit share) at current prices 1980–2006. Complements expenditure-side shares from earlier chunks.
- **Table 20.36** (growth rates): Useful for checking internal consistency and inflation dynamics by component

The labor share series (compensation of employees as % of GDP) shows the well-known Moos/structural shift: declining through the 1980s reform era, temporarily elevated in early 1990s, declining again post-2001 stabilization.

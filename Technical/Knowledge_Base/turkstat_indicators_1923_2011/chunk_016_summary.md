## Chapter 17 Explanation Page (p.538)
Covers methodology for TurkStat WPI indices (1981=100, 1987=100, 1994=100 bases), PPI from 2003, and CPI history from 1927 base through 1978-1979 base. Explains that older 1948=100 and 1963=100 WPI series are not continued in this publication.

## Chapter 18 Explanation Page (p.610)
Definitions of money stock concepts: M1 (narrow = currency + demand deposits), M2 (broad = M1 + time deposits + certificates), deposit types (official, commercial, interbank, savings), inflation adjustment notes for 2002–2004 period.

## Key Observations
- GDP deflator peaked at 107% annual inflation in 1994, 81% in 1997, then rapid disinflation post-2001 IMF program to single digits by 2004
- WPI series discontinued after 2010; PPI-based continuation from January 2006
- CPI clothing index shows near-zero to negative change 2004–2009 (structural shift, import competition)
- Building construction costs fell 4-5% in 2009 (GFC impact), strong recovery 2011 (+12%)

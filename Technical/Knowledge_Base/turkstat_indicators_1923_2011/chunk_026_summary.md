## Key Data Points (ESA-95 series)
- Nominal GDP grew from 70.2 trillion TL (1998) to 1,298.1 trillion TL (2011)
- Real GDP (1998 prices): 70.2 trillion (1998) → 114.9 trillion (2011)
- Real GDP growth: -4.8% (2009 recession), +9.2% (2010 recovery), +8.5% (2011)
- Household consumption stable at 66–72% of GDP
- GFCF rose from 22.9% of GDP (1998, current prices) to 21.8% (2011), with peak of 22.3% (2006)
- Private GFCF share of GFCF rose from 80% (1998) to 83% (2011)
- Exports as % of GDP: 21.3% (1998) → 23.7% (2011)

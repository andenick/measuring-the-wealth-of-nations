## Key Data Points for ST2 NickyData

### Hours Worked (Table 11.7) — ST2 Relevance: MODERATE
- Turkey 2012: of 24,821k employed, 24,093k (97%) were at work in reference week
- Male full-time intensity: majority work 41–71 hrs/week; standard-40-hour band = 2,356k males (2012)
- Overwork prevalent: 60-71 hrs band = 3,921k males; 50-59 = 2,950k (2012). Long hours signal wage compression relative to hours
- Female part-time: 17-35 hrs band = 1,700k (24% of employed females 2012), largely in agriculture

### Regional Labour Markets (Tables 11.8, 11.9) — ST2 Relevance: LOW-MODERATE
- Istanbul LFPR 51.1%, unemployment 11.3% (highest among large regions), employment rate 45.3%
- Southeast gap: TRC3 (Mardin/Batman/Sirnak/Siirt) unemployment 21.3%, employment rate only 27.5%
- Istanbul/Izmir/Ankara = high services share (62-73%); eastern Anatolia = high agriculture share (40-66%)

### Wage Employees by Province (Table 11.14) — ST2 CRITICAL
- **National total (2 Oct 2011)**: 24,320k employed; **16,664k regular/casual employees** (68.5%); 4,925k employer+self-employed; 2,731k unpaid family
- Istanbul: 3,919k wage employees out of 4,565k (85.8%) — key metropolitan wage share
- Ankara: 1,343k / 1,570k (85.5%); Kocaeli: 434k / 502k (86.5%) — industrial wage centres
- Eastern regions (Ardahan, Agri, Mus): low wage shares (29–40%), high unpaid family worker shares

### Collective Bargaining (Table 11.16) — ST2 RELEVANT for wage setting context
- Workers covered by collective agreements: 262,786 (2008) → 504,796 (2009 spike) → 338,671 (2010) → 422,802 (2011) → 231,863 (2012)
- Sharp drop in 2012: only 231,863 workers covered, lowest in series. Private sector 128,768; Public 103,095
- Bargaining universe: 103 labour unions, 47 employer unions (2012); manufacturing dominates (64 labour unions)

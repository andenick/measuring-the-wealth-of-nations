## Tables Extracted

**Table 26.8 (continued) — Business enterprises R&D personnel by economic activity and occupation, 2011**
- File: `chunk_014_table01_business_RD_personnel_by_activity_occupation_2011_continued.csv`
- Coverage: NACE sectors 65-66, 69-75, 77-82, 84, 85, 86-93, 94-96; headcount (A) and FTE (B)
- Key finding: Professional/scientific/technical activities (69-75) dominated with 5,983 headcount and 5,441 FTE; architectural/engineering/R&D subcategory (71-72) largest sub-group with 5,271 headcount

**Table 26.9 — Gross domestic expenditure on R&D in selected countries, 2007–2011 (PPP)**
- File: `chunk_014_table02_GERD_selected_countries_2007_2011_PPP.csv`
- Unit: Million current PPP US$
- Source: OECD Main Science and Technology Indicators, Volume 2012/2
- Coverage: 12 countries (Turkey, US, Germany, Austria, France, UK, Spain, Italy, Japan, Netherlands, Canada, Norway)
- Key finding: Turkey's GERD (PPP) rose from $7,048M to $11,101M (2007–2011); US remained far largest at ~$415B in 2011; Turkey's scale comparable to Norway/Austria but much smaller than Germany ($91.7B) or France ($51.9B)

**Table 26.10 — Proportion of computer and internet use by gender, 2012**
- File: `chunk_014_table03_computer_internet_use_by_gender_2012.csv`
- Coverage: Computer users and internet users by timing of last use; total/male/female
- Key finding: 48.7% used computer, 47.4% used internet; male usage (59.0%) substantially higher than female (38.5%); 51.3% had never used a computer

**Table 26.11 — Individuals using computer and internet in last 3 months (Jan-Mar 2012)**
- File: `chunk_014_table04_ICT_use_last_3months_by_demographics_2012.csv`
- Coverage: By age group (16-74), education level, and labour force status; computer and internet separately by gender
- Key finding: Youth (16-24) had 68.5% computer usage; students 90.7%; higher education 93.3%; employers (77.1%) and regular employees (67.1%) were more digitally active than self-employed (32.3%) or unpaid family workers (17.8%)

**Table 26.12 — Availability of ICT equipment in households, 2012**
- File: `chunk_014_table05_ICT_equipment_households_2012.csv`
- Coverage: 5 device types — desktop, portable computer, handheld computer, mobile phone, games console
- Key finding: Mobile phone penetration 93.2%; desktop 31.8%; laptop/tablet 27.1%; only 20.3% of households had internet-capable mobile phones

**Table 26.13 — Internet activities for private purposes, last 3 months (Jan-Mar 2012)**
- File: `chunk_014_table06_internet_activities_private_purposes_2012.csv`
- Coverage: 14 activity types by Turkey total, urban, rural
- Key finding: Reading online news was most common (72.5%); emailing 66.8%; finding product information 61.3%; internet banking only 17.1%; creating websites 5.0%; rural usage was consistently lower for most activities except gaming

## Methodological Notes

- ICT usage data reference period: January–March 2012 (for within-last-3-months figures)
- Labour force status for ICT tables uses reference period 26 March – 1 April 2012
- Age range for ICT data: 16–74 years
- Urban/rural breakdown in Table 26.13
- R&D personnel: A = Headcount, B = FTE (Full-Time Equivalent)
- OECD GERD data sourced from OECD Main Science and Technology Indicators, Vol. 2012/2
- Several country figures carry caveats: national estimates/projections, provisional data, breaks in series, exclusion of capital expenditures (USA)

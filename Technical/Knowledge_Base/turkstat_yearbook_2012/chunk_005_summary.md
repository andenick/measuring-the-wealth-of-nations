# chunk_005 Summary
**Source**: TurkStat Turkey's Statistical Yearbook 2012  
**Yearbook pages**: 218–227  
**Chapter**: 13 — İş İstatistikleri / Business Statistics  

## Content Overview

This chunk covers the **Business Statistics** chapter, consisting primarily of:
1. Methodology/explanation pages (pp. 218–219): Definitions of building statistics concepts (building floor area, m² unit prices, construction cost indices), and descriptions of quarterly construction sector indices (employment, hours worked, gross wages and salaries, turnover, production).
2. Three major data tables (pp. 220–227).

## Tables Extracted

### Table 13.1 — Employment and basic indicators by section in industry and service sectors, 2010
`CSV_Tables/chunk_005_table01_industry_services_employment_indicators_2010.csv`

- **Coverage**: All NACE Rev.2 industries and services, Turkey, reference year 2010.
- **Indicators**: Number of enterprises, persons employed, number of employees (ücretli çalışanlar), **wages and salaries (maaş ve ücretler)**, turnover, total purchases of goods/services, production value, value added at factor cost, gross investment in tangible goods.
- **Relevance to ST2/Labor Share**: HIGH. Contains total economy wages and salaries for 2010 across all sectors. General total wages = **TL 123,326,676,417**. Total persons employed = **10,197,331**; employees (ücretli) = **7,883,750**.
- Notable sector wage totals (TL):
  - Manufacturing: 43,181,084,139
  - Construction: 8,138,702,588
  - Wholesale/Retail: 22,531,329,436
  - Transportation: 10,918,706,673

### Table 13.2 — Employment and basic indicators in financial sectors, 2007–2011
`CSV_Tables/chunk_005_table02_financial_sector_employment_indicators_2007_2011.csv`

- **Coverage**: Financial and insurance activities (NACE Rev.2 K), sub-divided into financial services, insurance/reinsurance/pension, and auxiliary activities. Annual data 2007–2011.
- **Indicators**: Number of enterprises, annual average persons engaged, annual average employees, personnel cost, purchases of goods/services, production value, value added at factor cost.
- **Relevance to ST2/Labor Share**: MODERATE. Personnel cost (includes gross wages + social security) for the financial sector, 2007–2011 series. Useful for sectoral decomposition but not a direct national accounts total.
- Personnel cost trend (financial + insurance total, TL):
  - 2007: 9,771,497,668
  - 2008: 12,120,315,902
  - 2009: 13,530,527,611
  - 2010: 14,749,749,261
  - 2011: 17,206,192,162
- Source: TurkStat, Financial Intermediary Institution Statistics.

### Table 13.3 — Some of basic indicators by type of broadcasting, 2010–2011
`CSV_Tables/chunk_005_table03_broadcasting_indicators_2010_2011.csv`

- **Coverage**: Programming and broadcasting activities (NACE Rev.2), broken down by broadcast type (national/regional/local/satellite-cable-internet). 2010 and 2011 data.
- **Indicators**: Number of institutions, annual average employees/persons engaged, personnel cost, purchases, turnover, production value, value added at factor cost.
- **Relevance to ST2/Labor Share**: LOW. Sector-specific (media/broadcasting), not national accounts aggregate. Not useful for labor share or compensation of employees series.
- Source: TurkStat, Radio and Television Institution Statistics.

## Assessment for 2007–2012 National Accounts Target

- **No GSYH (GDP) data** present in this chunk.
- **No compensation of employees (işgücü ödemeleri/ücretler)** in national accounts format — the wages here are from business surveys (SBS/short-term business statistics), not from national income accounts.
- Table 13.1 wages figure for 2010 (total economy business sector: TL 123.3 billion) is valuable as a cross-check against national accounts compensation of employees, but is **not the same concept** (excludes public sector, self-employed, agriculture).

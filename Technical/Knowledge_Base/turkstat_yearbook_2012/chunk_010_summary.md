## CRITICAL ST2 NOTE: Income Approach GDP NOT PRESENT

This chunk contains the **chapter contents page and tables 21.1–21.13** of the National Accounts chapter. The chapter table of contents (page 347) lists only production-method and expenditure-method GDP tables — there is **NO table for GDP by income approach (Gelir yöntemiyle GSYH)** in this chunk or in the chapter table of contents as shown.

The income-approach breakdown (compensation of employees, operating surplus, mixed income, taxes on production) was calculated from 1996 onward per the methodology note, but no income-approach table appears in the visible chapter contents. This suggests the income approach table may be in a later chunk, or was not included in this yearbook's chapter 21.

**The expenditure-side table 21.8 does contain "Compensation of employees" as a sub-item of Government final consumption expenditure** — this is not factor income but rather government wage bill, and is captured in chunk_010_table08.

### Page 348 — National Accounts Methodology Explanation
Key methodological points:
- National income estimation started 1951 (National Income Study Group)
- GDP calculated by expenditure approach since 1993, income approach since 1996
- 1998 base year series started 2004, released March 2008 covering 1998–2007
- SNA 93 and ESA 95 recommendations used
- Current series uses production (by activity) and expenditure approaches

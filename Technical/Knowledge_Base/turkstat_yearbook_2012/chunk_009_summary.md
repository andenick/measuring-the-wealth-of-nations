## Content Overview

### Page 306 — Money, Banking and Insurance: Definitions
Text-only page. Provides bilingual (Turkish/English) definitions for key monetary concepts used in subsequent chapters:
- Gold reserves (international-standard and non-standard gold at Central Bank)
- Foreign exchange accounts and reserve tranche position
- Coins, securities portfolio, domestic credits, open market operations
- Foreign credits extended by Central Bank (Sudan, Albania agreements)
- Claims of Treasury (SDR quota gold/FX, various Treasury claims)
- Claims under legal proceedings (Iraq Central Bank / Bekhme Dam project)
- Deposits at Central Bank (public sector, banking, international institutions)
- Money and banking statistics scope: Central Bank, deposit banks, investment/development banks, participation banks, money market funds (Type-B liquid funds)
- Money supply definitions: M1 (currency + demand deposits TRY+FX), M2 (M1 + time deposits), M3 (M2 + repos + money market funds + debt securities issued)
- Balance of payments definition

### Page 338 — Finance Data Tables

#### Table 20.1: Special Provincial Administrations Budget Realizations, 2008–2012
- Unit: Thousand TL
- Coverage: All Special Provincial Administrations (Il Özel Idareleri), Turkey total
- Expenditures rose from TL 6.84bn (2008) to TL 11.52bn (2012)
- Key line: Compensation of employees ~TL 2.0–2.4bn throughout
- Revenues exceeded expenditures most years; budget surplus in 2010 (TL 1.10bn) and 2011 (TL 1.46bn)
- **CSV**: chunk_009_table01_special_provincial_administrations_budget_2008_2012.csv

#### Table 20.2: Municipalities Budget Realizations, 2008–2012
- Unit: Thousand TL
- Expenditures rose from TL 31.0bn (2008) to TL 47.0bn (2012)
- Compensation of employees: TL 5.9bn → TL 8.2bn
- Budget deficit throughout; worst in 2008 (TL -5.22bn), improved by 2010 (TL -241m)
- **CSV**: chunk_009_table02_municipalities_budget_2008_2012.csv

### Page 339 — Finance Data Tables (continued)

#### Table 20.3: Municipalities and Special Provincial Budgets by Statistical Region (IBBS), 2012
- Municipalities expenditures: Istanbul dominates at 28.8% (TL 13.5bn); Ankara second at 9.5%
- Special provincial: most spending in eastern/southeastern regions (Van/Mus/Bitlis/Hakkari: 6.2%; Trabzon region: 5.8%)
- **CSV**: chunk_009_table03_municipalities_budget_by_SR_2012.csv

#### Table 20.4: Central Government Budget Realizations, 2008–2012
- Unit: Thousand TL
- Expenditures: TL 227bn (2008) → TL 360bn (2012)
- Revenues: TL 210bn (2008) → TL 332bn (2012)
- Budget deficit widened dramatically in 2009 (TL -52.8bn) then improved
- Primary balance (excluding interest): positive throughout except 2009 (TL +440m near-zero)
- **CSV**: chunk_009_table04_central_government_budget_2008_2012.csv

### Page 341 — Finance Data Tables (continued)

#### Table 20.6: Gross External Debt of Turkey by Lender, 2008–2012
- Unit: Million USD
- Total external debt: $281bn (2008) → $337bn (2012)
- Short-term debt surged: $52bn (2008) → $101bn (2012)
- Long-term debt slight decline then recovery: $229bn → $236bn
- Official creditors (long-term): $34bn → $42bn; multilateral organizations dominant within official
- Bonds (external): $39bn → $64bn
- **CSV**: chunk_009_table05_gross_external_debt_by_lender_2008_2012.csv

#### Table 20.7: Central Government Domestic Debt Stock, 2008–2012
- Unit: Million TL
- Total: TL 275bn → TL 387bn
- Almost entirely government bonds; treasury bills eliminated by 2011 (zero), small issuance resumed 2012
- **CSV**: chunk_009_table06_central_govt_domestic_debt_2008_2012.csv

### Page 342 — Finance Data Table

#### Table 20.8: Balance of Payments, 2008–2012
- Unit: Million USD
- Current account deficit peaked at $75bn (2011), improved to $47bn (2012)
- Trade deficit: dominant driver; peaked at $89bn goods deficit (2011)
- Services surplus: persistent positive ~$17–24bn (tourism-driven)
- Income account: persistently negative (-$6.7 to -$8.4bn); compensation of employees small (-$66m to -$215m)
- Financial account: large inflows; portfolio investment $40.8bn in 2012
- **CSV**: chunk_009_table07_balance_of_payments_2008_2012.csv

### Page 343 — Finance Data Tables

#### Table 20.9: Companies/Cooperatives Established/Liquidated, 2008–2012
- Newly established peaked at 54,442 (2011), dropped to 39,764 (2012)
- Liquidations rising: 9,578 (2008) → 16,063 (2012)
- Capital in newly established: TL 10.2bn (2009) → TL 27.4bn (2011)

## Methodology Notes (from pp. 211–217 — no data tables)

Key methodological information relevant to interpreting Chapter 13 tables:

- **Annual Business Statistics** (Yıllık İş İstatistikleri): NACE Rev.2 section-level; covers all sectors from mining through other services. Does NOT include agriculture or public administration.
- **Wages and salaries** (Maaş ve ücretler): Defined as total remuneration in cash or kind payable to persons on payroll (including homeworkers) for work done. Excludes social security — this is the pure wage bill.
- **Personnel costs** (Personel maliyetleri): Gross payments to personnel PLUS social security costs (broader than wages alone).
- **Industrial Labour Input Indices** (base year 2010=100): Cover employment, hours worked, and gross wages-salaries quarterly; NACE Rev.2 sectors B (mining), C (manufacturing), D (electricity), E-36 (water supply).
- **Trade and Services Indices** (base year 2005=100): Turnover, employment, hours worked, gross wages-salaries; quarterly, NACE Rev.2.
- **Construction Statistics**: Based on building permit and occupancy permit forms; covers buildings only (not roads, dams, bridges).

# chunk_003 Summary — turkstat_yearbook_2012

**Pages covered**: 174–183 (Chapter 11: Çalışma / Labour)
**Yearbook**: TurkStat Turkey's Statistical Yearbook 2012

## Content Overview

This chunk covers the **Labour** chapter methodology notes and six statistical tables on employment and labour force, covering **2008–2012**.

### Pages 174–177: Definitions and Methodology
- Labour statistics methodology: definitions of employed, unemployed, labour force, LFPR, unemployment rate, employment status categories (ICSE 1993), economic activities (NACE Rev.2)
- 2011 Population and Housing Census (NKA/PHC) methodology: reference date 2 October 2011, field work 3 Oct–31 Dec 2011, sample = 22,861 enumeration areas (11.7% of 195,192), ~8.1 million persons in 2.2 million households
- Wage definition: average daily wages include premiums, overtime, severance pay; gross wages calculated on insurance deduction base

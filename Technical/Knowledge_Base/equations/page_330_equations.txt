Trade and Wage Calculation Equations:

1. $ec_{whtr} = (EC / FEE)_{whtr}$ - Wholesale trade employee compensation per full-time equivalent

2. $W_{whtr} = ec_{whtr} \times L_{whtr}$ - Wholesale trade wages

3. $ec_{retir} = (EC / FEE)_{retir}$ - Retail trade employee compensation per full-time equivalent

4. $W_{retir} = ec_{retir} \times L_{retir}$ - Retail trade wages

5. $W_{br} = W_{re} - W_{gr}$ - Building rental wages = Real estate wages - Ground rent wages

6. $EC_{fi} = EC_{fire} - EC_{re}$ - Finance/insurance employee compensation = Finance/insurance/real estate compensation - Real estate compensation

7. $FEE_{fi} = FEE_{fire} - FEE_{re}$ - Finance/insurance full-time equivalents = Finance/insurance/real estate FTE - Real estate FTE

8. $ec_{fi} = (EC / FEE)_{fi}$ - Finance/insurance employee compensation per full-time equivalent

9. $L_{fi} = L_{fire} - L_{re}$ - Finance/insurance labor = Finance/insurance/real estate labor - Real estate labor

10. $W_{fi} = ec_{fi} \times L_{fi}$ - Finance/insurance wages

11. $ec_{re} = (EC / FEE)_{re}$ - Real estate employee compensation per full-time equivalent

12. $W_{re} = ec_{re} \times L_{re}$ - Real estate wages

13. $W_{gr} = W_{re} \times g$ - Ground rent wages = Real estate wages × proportion of ground rent

14. $W_{govtot} = EC_{govfed} + EC_{govst}$ - Total government wages = Federal government compensation + State/local government compensation

Where:
- EC = Employee compensation
- FEE = Full-time equivalent employees
- L = Labor
- W = Wages
- g = Proportion of ground rent in real estate (from Table B.5)
- whtr = Wholesale trade
- retir = Retail trade
- br = Building rental
- fi = Finance/insurance
- fire = Finance/insurance/real estate
- re = Real estate
- gr = Ground rent
- govfed = Federal government
- govst = State/local government

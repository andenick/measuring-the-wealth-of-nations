\section*{Government Expenditures in National Accounts}

\subsection*{Key Equation:}
\[
G = G' + W_G
\]

Where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $G$: Total government expenditures
    \item $G'$: Government purchases of goods and services (including royalty payments)
    \item $W_G$: Wages paid to government employees
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{IO-NIPA Treatment:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item $G'$ is treated as part of final use (under final demand)
    \item Net interest and ground rent are treated as purchases from royalties sector
    \item $W_G$ is treated as purchases of government services
    \item A dummy "government industry" is added to IO table
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Theoretical Issue:}
Inconsistent treatment of government components:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $G'$ as final demand implies nonproduction activity
    \item But $W_G$ treated as intermediate input to "government industry"
    \item This creates theoretical inconsistency in national accounting
\end{itemize}

## Abstract

The main focus of this study is the role of the state in the distribution process vis-a-vis the working class in the United States during the period 1952-1980.

**Central Research Question**:
What is the net impact of the distributive activities of the state on the wages of the working class as a whole and that of productive workers?

**Methodological Approach**:

To address this question, the study proposes two concepts:

1. **Net Tax**: Taxes paid to the state **minus** benefits and income received from it.

2. **Rate of Surplus-Value (Adjusted)**: Posed by:
   - Narrowing down the concept of net tax paid by labor
   - Determining the portion of net tax paid exclusively by productive workers

**Key Finding**:
The empirical findings concerning both the net tax paid by labor and the adjusted rate of surplus-value suggest that **claiming the existence of a welfare state in the United States is highly questionable**.

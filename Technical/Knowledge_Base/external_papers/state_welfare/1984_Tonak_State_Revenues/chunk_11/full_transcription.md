One way of checking this possibility of overestima-tion is to compare out labor shares with the percentages of taxes paid by certain income groups. For example, in 1980 taxable returns with the annual adjusted gross income up to $49,999 contributed 68.9 per cent of the total federal income taxes (U.S. Bureau of the Census, 1982, p. 257). As noted earlier, we used a figure of 71 per cent of federal income taxes as the working class share based on the procedure described previously. The figure we now get, based on a cutoff point of household incomes of less than $50,000, is roughly 69 per cent. But this latter figure refers to household income not merely to individual income. Therefore it is quite plausible that at least some have incomes which in 1980 fall in the range of $50,000 and above.

On this basis, our earlier figure of 71 per cent seems perfectly adequate, and certainly does not suggest any overestimation of the tax share paid by the working class.

## Key Content Summary

### Methodology Discussion (Pages 90-94)

1. **Labor Share Calculation**: Ratio of total labor income to adjusted personal income used as allocation coefficient

2. **Validation**: Comparison with Weisskopf's estimates shows Tonak's method is conservative (page 91)

3. **Income Threshold Test**: 1980 IRS data shows 68.9% of federal income taxes paid by households earning under $50,000, supporting Tonak's 71% labor share estimate (page 94)

4. **Robustness**: Tonak argues his labor share estimates are adequate and unlikely to overestimate taxes paid by workers

### Tax Allocation Rules (Pages 94-96)

**Direct to Labor (T₁)**:
- Contributions for Social Insurance (100%)
- Government lotteries/parimutuels (negative revenue, attributed to workers)

**Direct to Non-Labor (T₃)**:
- Estate and Gift Taxes (100%)
- Corporate Profit Taxes (100%)
- Indirect Business Taxes (100%)

**Mixed Allocation (T₂)** - by labor share:
- Personal Income Taxes
- Property Taxes (with NIPA correction for homeowners)
- Motor Vehicle Licenses
- Personal non-taxes and other taxes

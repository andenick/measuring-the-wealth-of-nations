### Expected Findings

Based on Abstract: "net impact of distributive activities...claiming existence of welfare state...highly questionable"

This suggests Tonak found:
- **Negative Net Tax** for most years (workers pay more than they receive)
- **Welfare state myth**: State does not subsidize workers, workers subsidize state
- This aligns with our Phase 1 finding: NSW was negative for most of postwar period

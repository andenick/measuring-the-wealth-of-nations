## Context

This section addresses **debated issues** in applying PUPL distinction:

1. **Intrinsic qualities** - Does the nature of the product (weapons vs. medicine) determine if labor is productive?
2. **Materiality** - Must productive labor create material/tangible commodities?
3. **Circulation sphere** - Are commercial workers productive?
4. **Individual vs. activity** - Can same person be both productive and unproductive?
5. **Domestic labor** - Is household labor productive?
6. **Military goods** - Is weapons production productive labor?
7. **"Indirectly productive"** - Can state workers be indirectly productive?

All debates are resolved through textual argument, not visual aids.

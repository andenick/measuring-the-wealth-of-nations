- Marx's 1867 preface: "ultimate aim... to lay bare the economic law of motion of modern society"
- Why singular "law" not plural "laws"?

  1. Proletarian vs. bourgeois perspective
  2. Capitalism as temporary vs. eternal
- Russian author's review (quoted approvingly by Marx): Marx's method is dialectical
- "Of still greater moment" = law of variation, development, transition from one form to another
- "The scientific value... lies in disclosing... death of given social organism and replacement by another and higher one"
- Marx's endorsement: this is "the dialectic method"
- **Conclusion**: The singular economic law of motion = capitalism producing communism

## Key Concepts and Findings

### Productive/Unproductive Labour Theory (Chapter 5 Conclusion)
1. **Hierarchy of concepts**: Productive labour in general → Productive labour for capital
2. **Direct vs. indirect**: Only direct creation of surplus-value counts as productive
3. **No double counting**: Productivity increases by scientists/teachers already counted in production workers' surplus-value
4. **Wage labour criterion**: Distinction only applies to wage workers
5. **Analytical purpose**: For studying accumulation, not moral judgment

### Capital as Book of Communism (Chapter 6 Opening)
1. **Political character**: Not merely economic analysis
2. **Historical transition**: Study of capitalism's self-negation
3. **Singular law**: Production of communism by capitalism
4. **Dialectical method**: Endorsed through Russian reviewer's interpretation
5. **Revolutionary implications**: Provides criteria for assessing ripeness for socialism

### Methodological Insights
1. **Dialectical approach**: Things transform into their opposites (fundamental characteristics → opposites)
2. **Historical specificity**: Capitalism as temporary, not eternal
3. **Class perspective**: Proletarian standpoint vs. bourgeois political economy
4. **Scientific objectivity**: Studying necessity of transition, not just current system

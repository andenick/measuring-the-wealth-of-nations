## Major Theoretical Arguments

### Chapter 5 Conclusion (Pages 115-119)

1. **Hierarchy of Productive Labour Concepts**:
   - Productive labour in general (foundation)
   - Productive labour for capital (specific to capitalism)

2. **Direct vs. Indirect Productivity**:
   - Only labour that directly creates surplus-value is productive
   - Scientists, educators, health workers who increase productivity are NOT separately productive
   - Their contribution already counted in production workers' surplus-value
   - Counting it separately = "double counting"

3. **Classification Criteria**:
   - Distinction applies only to wage labour
   - Excludes: housewives, self-sufficient peasants, petty commodity producers
   - Purpose: Analyze accumulation variables, not moral judgment

### Chapter 6 Opening (Pages 121-125)

1. **Main Thesis**:
   - Capital is the source for Lenin and Trotsky's criteria for revolutionary maturity
   - Capital is not just economics but a political work
   - Capital is not just about capitalism but about communism

2. **The Singular Economic Law**:
   - Marx speaks of "THE economic law of motion" (singular)
   - Not just plural "laws of motion" (falling profit rate, etc.)
   - **THE law** = capitalism producing communism

3. **Marx's Dialectical Method**:
   - Shows necessity of present order
   - Shows necessity of another order into which first "must inevitably pass"
   - Studies "death of given social organism and replacement by another and higher one"
   - Endorsed through Russian reviewer's interpretation

4. **Distinction from Political Economy**:
   - Political economy: bourgeois, sees capitalism as eternal
   - Marx's critique: proletarian, sees capitalism as temporary

## Critical Quotes

### On Productive Labour
> "...labour is only productive, and an exponent of labour-power is only a productive worker, if it or he creates surplus-value directly, i.e. the only productive labour is that which is directly consumed in the course of production for the valorization of capital" (Marx, 1976a, 1038a)

### On Capital's Purpose
> "...it is the ultimate aim of this work, to lay bare the economic law of motion of modern society..." (Marx, 1968, 10)

### On the Dialectical Method
> "The scientific value of such an inquiry lies in the disclosing of the special laws that regulate the origin, existence, development, death of a given social organism and its replacement by another and higher one" (Russian author, endorsed by Marx)

### On Revolutionary Ripeness
> "All talk to the effect that historical conditions have not yet 'ripened' for socialism is the product of ignorance or conscious deception." (Trotsky, 1977, 112)

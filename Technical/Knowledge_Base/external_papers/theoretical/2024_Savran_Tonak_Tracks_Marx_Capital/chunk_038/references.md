## Key Terms and Concepts

### Marxist Terminology
- **Valorisation** (*Verwertung* in German): Process whereby production adds new, additional value to the object of labour through expenditure of living labour
- **Devalorisation** (*Entwertung* in German): Loss in value, which can reach loss of entire value; elimination of old productive capacity
- **Surplus-value**: Additional value that workers produce beyond reproduction of the value of their wages
- **Fictitious capital**: Capital that separates from original capital it represents and can take flight towards magnitudes of value incommensurate with original productive capital
- **Over-credit**: Credit and finance carry economic activity beyond confines of what is rationally possible given capacity of production system
- **Simple reproduction**: Capitalist class consumes entire surplus-value as revenue
- **Expanded reproduction**: Capital accumulation itself; capital expands its scale of production by using surplus-value to purchase new means of production and hire new workers
- **Moral depreciation**: Means of production becoming obsolete, no longer competitive

### Economic Theories
- **Say's Law**: Demand is created by supply; whoever sells buys
- **Profit-squeeze theory**: Crisis caused by strengthening of labour and consequent rise in wages
- **Underconsumption theory**: Lack of demand as cause of crises
- **Law of the tendential fall of the profit rate**: Crisis explanation based on changing composition of capital
- **Creative destruction**: Schumpeter's concept
- **Reserve army of labour**: Unemployed workers

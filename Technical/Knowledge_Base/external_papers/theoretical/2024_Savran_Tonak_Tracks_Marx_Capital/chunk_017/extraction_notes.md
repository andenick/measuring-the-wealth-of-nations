## Argumentative Structure

### Main Thesis
**Central Argument**: The most important conclusion of Marx's Capital is that **capitalism generates communism** through the socialization of productive forces, and this thesis forms an indissoluble unity with Marx's entire worldview from 1845 onwards.

### Supporting Arguments

1. **Historical Continuity** (Section 5):
   - Capital provides scientific proof for theses already stated in 1848 Communist Manifesto
   - 1845 German Ideology established communism as "real movement"
   - 1859 Contribution formulated historical materialism
   - 1867 Capital demonstrates these in detail through critique of political economy

2. **Theoretical Unity**:
   - Capital and Communist Manifesto in "perfect harmony"
   - Verification of historical materialism
   - Refutation of utopian socialism's idealist approach

3. **World-Historical Significance** (Section 6):
   - First time in history individual interest better served by collective organization
   - Material bases of class struggle disappearing
   - New relationship between individual and collective

4. **Dialectical Property Relations**:
   - Negation of negation: small property → capitalist private property → individual property on basis of common property
   - Aufhebung: conserves "acquisitions of capitalist era" while transcending capitalist relations

### Polemical Targets

1. **Economists who ignore/cannot grasp** Capital's central proposition
2. **David Harvey** - accused of treating Communist Manifesto as mere "rhetoric"
3. **Bourgeois ideology** - assumes individual self-interest precludes communism
4. **Those who don't grasp "Historical Tendency" chapter** - haven't understood Capital or Marx-Engels thinking

### Rhetorical Strategies

- **Strong declarative statements**: "Lenin obviously understated his case"

- Dialectical progression: individual small-scale property → capitalist private property → individual property based on common property
- "Negation of the negation"
- First negation: separation of producer from means of production
- Second negation: expropriation of expropriators
- Result: individual property BUT on basis of common ownership of means of production
- "Based on acquisitions of capitalist era" - Aufhebung (supersession AND conservation)
- Not wholesale negation of capitalism but preservation of its achievements
- Marx's phrase present in multiple editions (German, French, English, Russian) - not error

**Philosophical Framework**: Hegelian dialectics - Aufhebung, negation of negation, synthesis

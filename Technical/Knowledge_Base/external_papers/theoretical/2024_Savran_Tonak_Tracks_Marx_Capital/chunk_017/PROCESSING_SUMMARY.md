## Key Findings

### Central Thesis
The most important conclusion of Marx's Capital is that **capitalism generates communism** through the socialization of productive forces, and this forms an indissoluble unity with Marx's entire worldview from 1845 onwards.

### Major Arguments

1. **Historical Continuity**: Capital provides scientific proof for theses stated in 1848 Communist Manifesto and 1845 German Ideology

2. **Dialectical Synthesis**: Communism represents "individual property based on common property" - a negation of the negation preserving capitalism's "acquisitions"

3. **World-Historical Significance**: For first time in history, individual interest better served by collective methods than competition

4. **Revolutionary Character**: Capital is "as revolutionary a text as the Communist Manifesto" - theoretical and political unity

### Critical Concept: "Individual Property"

Marx's paradoxical phrase from Capital I, p. 763:
- NOT return to small-scale private property
- "Individual property based on...possession in common of...means of production"
- Aufhebung (Hegelian supersession and conservation)
- Collective interest as "highest mediation" of individual interest

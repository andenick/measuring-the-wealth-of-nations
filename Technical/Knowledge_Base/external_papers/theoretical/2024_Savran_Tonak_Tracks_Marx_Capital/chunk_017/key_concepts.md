# Key Concepts and Terms - Pages 136-145 (Book pages 161-170)

## Primary Theoretical Concepts

### 1. **Socialisation of Productive Forces**
- Central thesis: capitalism generates productive forces that enter into contradiction with capitalist relations of production
- Productive forces become "only usable in common"
- Creates material basis for transition from capitalism to communism

### 2. **Historical Materialism / Materialist Conception of History**
- Key to historical development found in "material conditions of life"
- Economic structure (relations of production) forms the "real foundation"
- Productive forces come into conflict with existing relations of production
- Relations turn from "forms of development" into "fetters"

### 3. **Negation of the Negation** (Dialectical Process)
- First negation: destruction of unity between direct producer and means of production (primitive accumulation)
- Second negation: expropriation of the expropriators (socialist revolution)
- Results in "individual property based on common property"

### 4. **Individual vs. Collective Interest (Mediation)**
- **Key proposition**: For first time in history, individual interest can be better satisfied through collective methods than through competition
- Collective interest becomes "highest mediation of individual interest"
- Not rejection of individual interest but recognition that collective organization serves individual interest better

### 5. **Individual Property on Basis of Common Property**
- Dialectical synthesis (Aufhebung)
- "Individual property based on the acquisitions of the capitalist era"
- Based on "co-operation and the possession in common of the land and of the means of production"
- Both supersession AND conservation of capitalism's achievements

## Historical-Political Concepts

### 6. **Scientific Socialism vs. Utopian Socialism**
- Communism not an ideal to be established but "real movement which abolishes the present state of things"
- Conditions of movement result from "now existing premise" (capitalist mode of production)
- Differs from Saint-Simon, Owen, Fourier's idealistic approach

### 7. **Capital as Infrastructure for Communist Manifesto**
- Capital provides scientific proof for theses stated in 1848 Manifesto
- Two decades of research (1848-1867) substantiate earlier claims
- "Capital is as revolutionary a text as the Communist Manifesto"

### 8. **Continuity 1845-1848-1859-1867**
- 1845: German Ideology - communism as real movement
- 1848: Communist Manifesto - productive forces revolt against property relations
- 1859: Contribution to Critique of Political Economy - formulation of historical materialism
- 1867: Capital Volume I - scientific proof and detailed demonstration

## Economic-Social Concepts

### 9. **Contradiction Between Socialization and Private Appropriation**
- Socialization of means of production and labour
- Private nature of appropriation under capitalism
- Manifests concretely through economic crises (per author's interpretation)

### 10. **"War of Each Against All"** (Engels)
- Capitalism dissolves traditional protective mechanisms
- Naked cash nexus replaces communal bonds
- Bourgeois individualism as ideological norm
- Social Darwinism applied to society

### 11. **Instruments of Labour Only Usable in Common**
- Key phrase from "Historical Tendency of Capitalist Accumulation"
- Capitalism creates productive forces requiring collective organization
- Technical/organizational level makes individual/private use impossible or inefficient

## Philosophical-Methodological Concepts

### 12. **Aufhebung** (Hegelian Dialectics)
- Simultaneous supersession and conservation
- Applied to transition from capitalism to communism
- Preserves "acquisitions of capitalist era" while transcending capitalist property relations

### 13. **High Level of Abstraction**
- Capital's propositions formulated at high abstraction level
- World-historical propositions, not immediate empirical descriptions
- Requires concrete analysis for manifestation in real conditions

### 14. **Material Bases of Struggle Over Interests**
- Throughout class society: conflicts arise from material interests
- Under communism: material bases of these struggles disappear (first time in written history)
- Does NOT mean end of individual interest but end of antagonistic contradiction

## Class and Revolution Concepts

### 15. **Proletariat as Grave-Diggers**
- Capital produces both undermining productive forces AND revolutionary class
- "Involuntary combination due to association" replaces isolation
- Modern industry creates material and social basis for its own overthrow

### 16. **Expropriation of Expropriators**
- Capitalists expropriate small producers (first negation)
- Working class expropriates capitalists (second negation - "negation of negation")
- Dialectical return to producer's control but on new basis

## Critical Polemical Points

### 17. **Critique of David Harvey**
- Author criticizes Harvey for treating Communist Manifesto as mere "rhetoric"
- Insists Capital and Manifesto are in "perfect harmony"
- Both are revolutionary texts with same fundamental content

### 18. **Critique of Bourgeois Ideology**
- Views communism as doomed because of individual self-interest
- Assumes capitalism necessary for productive force development
- Misses that collective organization can better serve individual interest

### 19. **Vital Significance of "Historical Tendency" Chapter**
- Those who don't grasp this chapter haven't understood Capital OR Marx-Engels thinking
- "Lenin obviously understated his case" (stronger claim than Lenin's)
- Indissoluble unity with Marx's overall world comprehension

## Temporal Concepts

### 23. **New Epoch in Human History**
- First time since rise of class society / dawn of written history
- Material bases for conflicts of interest disappearing
- Individual-collective relationship fundamentally transformed
- Not end of history but qualitatively new stage

### 24. **Labour Productivity Unprecedented in History**
- Capitalism's achievement recognized by Marxists
- Creates opportunity for more prosperous, secure, fulsome life
- But capitalism becomes barrier to realization for majority

## Key Findings and Contributions

### Theoretical Innovations

1. **Two Sources of Profit Framework**
   - Profit on Transfers (trading profit, "buy cheap, sell dear")
   - Profit from Surplus-Value (extraction in production)

2. **Digital Labor Typology**
   - **Productive wage laborers**: Facebook employees who produce surplus-value
   - **Petty commodity producers**: Facebook users who produce value but not surplus-value
   - **Unproductive laborers**: Workers in finance/trade who facilitate surplus-value transfer

3. **Facebook Production Analysis**
   - Facebook workers create a "social interaction environment" as a tangible commodity
   - This environment is "enriched" by user content
   - The marketable environment is sold to advertisers
   - Surplus-value is realized through advertising sales

4. **Rate of Exploitation**
   - Defined as ratio of surplus labor time to necessary labor time
   - Applicable to both productive and unproductive workers
   - Different basis for exploitation in each category

### Empirical Contributions

1. **Crisis Vulnerability Analysis**
   - **Germany**: Resilient due to productive, tradeable central sectors
   - **Spain**: Vulnerable due to Construction dominance and non-tradeable sectors
   - **USA**: Vulnerable due to unproductive sector dominance

2. **Facebook Statistics** (as documented)

## Chapter 13: Lean Production and Flexibility - Theoretical Foundations

### Core Argument

This chunk completes the critique of "Fordism" and establishes lean production as capital's systematic response to assembly line contradictions. Savran demonstrates that lean production is fundamentally a labor intensification system disguised as efficiency improvement, centered on eliminating "idle time" through the Just-In-Time (JIT) mechanism.

### Key Theoretical Contributions

**1. Fordism Critique Completion**
- Fordism = Taylorism + moving assembly line (no independent conceptual status)
- Assembly line originated in slaughterhouses (systematic "disassembly")
- "Fordism" should be abandoned as vacuous concept; Taylorism restored as proper term

**2. Lean Production Definition**
- Most accurate term among alternatives (vs. post-Fordism, Toyotism, flexible production)
- Definition: production cleansed of all resource use beyond strict necessity
- Aim: consolidate capital control over labor, increase productivity/intensity, reduce constant capital costs
- Two dimensions: (a) labor process/worker management, (b) workplace fragmentation with supplier networks

**3. Assembly Line Contradictions** (driving lean production adoption)
- **Idle time**: balancing line problems create unused labor moments
- **Slowing necessity**: demand drops require pace reduction, creating more idle time
- **Worker knowledge loss**: Taylorism prevents utilizing worker experience
- **Waste**: JIC inventory systems tie up idle capital; post-production quality control allows prolonged defective production

**4. JIT System - The Theoretical Core**

**Conventional view (rejected):** Technical/financial method for inventory cost reduction

**Actual function:** Labor intensification through idle time identification/elimination

**Mechanism:**
- Production without inventory exposes unable workers/stations
- Andon (visualization) system: green/amber/red lights
- Paradox: constant green = bad (indicates surplus labor)
- Management heaps more work OR draws workers away until yellow-light limit reached
- Result: "management by stress/pressure" (Parker & Slaughter 1988)

**Taiichi Ohno's formula:** Present capacity = work + waste
- Goal: zero waste, 100% work percentage
- US Transportation Dept definition: "making each worker work 60 minutes every hour"

**Kaizen (continuous improvement):** Perpetual tempo increase, endless pressure intensification
- In Japan: workers arrive early, use breaks for precautionary work
- Creates new "work time" beyond contract ("elasticity of workday")

**5. Task Mobility and Interchangeability**
- Worker replaceability enables: (a) line rebalancing during tempo changes, (b) layoffs during slowdowns
- Modular system: U-form work stations with multiple machines per worker
- Workers perform multiple tasks endlessly (vs. single task endlessly in classical system)
- Production workers absorb maintenance and quality control functions
- Result: further idle time reduction through multi-tasking

### Marxist Framework Applications

**Surplus-Value Extraction:**
- Lean production targets relative surplus-value through labor intensity increase
- Eliminates "pores" in working day that classical Taylorism couldn't reach
- Extends working day beyond contract (absolute surplus-value component)

**Labor Process Control:**
- Extends Taylorism's expropriation of worker knowledge
- Paradoxically requires some skill broadening (task mobility) while maintaining deskilling
- Visualizes and quantifies previously invisible idle time

**Capital Accumulation:**
- Reduces circulating constant capital (inventory elimination)
- Increases rate of surplus-value (labor intensity)
- Dual contribution to profit rate

### Methodological Notes

**Critical approach:** Savran systematically dismantles mainstream lean production literature's technical/neutral framing, exposing class control mechanisms

**Historical materialism:** Locates lean production within capitalism's continuous drive to subordinate labor more completely to capital's valorization needs

**Concept precision:** Careful terminological analysis (Fordism vs. Taylorism, JIT's true vs. apparent function) reveals ideological mystifications in management literature

### Chapter 9:

- **Surplus-value**: Extra value created beyond wage equivalent
- **Rate of exploitation**: Surplus labour time / necessary labour time
- **Productive labour**: Labour employed by capital to produce surplus-value
- **Unproductive labour**: Labour not producing surplus-value but still exploited
- **PUPL distinction**: Productive and Unproductive Labour classification
- **Social reproduction**: Context for defining labour activity

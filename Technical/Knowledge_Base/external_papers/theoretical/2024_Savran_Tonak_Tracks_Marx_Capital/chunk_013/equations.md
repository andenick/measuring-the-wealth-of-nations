## Equation 1: Circuit of Capital

**Location**: Page 95 (book page), page 1 (PDF page)

**Context**: "The formula for the circuit of capital, M – C ... P ... C' – M' is the most general representation of the process of self-expansion (the process of valorization) of capital."

**LaTeX**:
```latex
M - C \ldots P \ldots C' - M'
```

**Alternative LaTeX (with spacing)**:
```latex
M - C \; \ldots \; P \; \ldots \; C' - M'
```

**Components**:
- **M**: Money capital (initial investment)
- **C**: Commodities purchased (labour-power and means of production)
- **P**: Production process (where surplus-value is created)
- **C'**: Commodities with higher value (after production)
- **M'**: Expanded money capital (M + surplus-value)

**Relationship**:
- M' > M (capital has expanded)
- M' - M = surplus-value
- Only during P (production) is surplus-value created
- C-M and M-C phases are circulation (no value creation)

**Theoretical Significance**:
This formula is central to distinguishing productive from unproductive labour. Only labour employed during the P (production) phase creates surplus-value. Labour employed in the M-C and C'-M' metamorphoses (circulation) is unproductive, as it only changes the form of capital without creating new value.

**Related Formulas** (mentioned in text):
- C - M (commodity to money transformation)
- M - C (money to commodity transformation)

These are the circulation phases where no surplus-value is produced.

## Notes on Mathematical Notation

The ellipsis (...) notation indicates:
- Interruption of the circulation process
- Entry into production sphere
- Time period during which production occurs
- Return to circulation sphere

The prime notation (') indicates:
- Greater value/magnitude
- C' contains more value than the original commodities C
- M' is larger than M by the amount of surplus-value extracted

### Key Theoretical Advances

**Four-Level Classification System**:
1. Commodity production (vs. use-value)
2. Wage-labour (vs. independent production)
3. Exchange against capital (vs. revenue)
4. Production sphere (vs. circulation)

**Only workers meeting ALL four criteria = productive**

**Critical Insights**:
- Circulation creates no value despite being "necessary"
- Transportation is production, not circulation
- Profits of circulation capital come from redistributed surplus-value
- Same worker may perform both productive and unproductive tasks
- Conceptual clarity distinct from empirical measurement challenges

## Figure 2: Profit upon Alienation

**Type**: Flowchart/Diagram

**Location**: Page 168 (printed page number 168)

**Full Caption**: "Fig. 2 Profit upon alienation"

**Description**:
A hierarchical flowchart showing two main branches of Profit upon Alienation (also labeled as "Profit upon Expropriation"):

### Left Branch: Real Estate (Privatization)
- **Starting point**: Public Land (sold for less than its value)
- **Intermediate step**: Private Land of capitalist developer
- **Two outcomes**:
  1. Private Land sold at its market value → **Profit upon Alienation** → leads to a decrease in public wealth & an increase in profit
  2. Private Land developed & sold at its market value → **Profit upon Alienation + Surplus Value** → leads to a decrease in public wealth & an increase in profit

### Right Branch: Banking (Lending to Workers)
- **Starting point**: Interest Paid by workers
- **Two pathways**:
  1. Interest Paid by productive workers (out of variable capital) → **Profit upon Alienation** → leads to a decrease in net wages & an increase in profit
  2. Interest Paid by unproductive workers (out of surplus value) → **Profit upon Alienation** → leads to a decrease in net wages & an increase in profit

**Key Insight**: The diagram illustrates how profit can be generated through transfers between the circuit of capital and other spheres (state, households) via unequal exchange, distinct from surplus-value creation in production.

**Visual Elements**:
- Boxes with labels and italicized descriptors
- Arrows showing flow and relationships
- Text below branches explaining economic consequences
- Hierarchical structure from general (top) to specific (bottom)

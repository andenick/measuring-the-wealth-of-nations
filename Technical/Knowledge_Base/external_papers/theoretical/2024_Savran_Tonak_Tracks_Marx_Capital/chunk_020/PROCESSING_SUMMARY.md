### Land Privatization (Turkey, 2010-2021)
- **Total POA Generated**: US$9.31 billion (nominal)
- **Mechanism**: State sells public land below market value
- **Data Source**: Directorate General of National Property (DGNP)
- **Methodology**: Market Price (MP) - Public Price (PP) × Area

### Worker Lending (Turkey, 2010-2021)
- **Total POA Generated**: US$57.23 billion (nominal, referenced)
- **Mechanism**: Banks profit from consumer loans to workers
- **Household Debt Growth**: 1.7% GDP (2002) → 8.9% GDP (2022)
- **Worker Share**: 70% of all consumer loans

### Combined Impact
- **Total POA (2 sectors)**: US$66.54 billion over 12 years
- **Average Annual POA**: US$5.55 billion/year
- **Significance**: Represents major channel of profit beyond surplus-value creation

# Dialectical Concepts in Capital - Summary from Chunk 008

**Source**: Savran & Tonak (2024), Chapter 3, Section 3 continuation and throughout (pp. 43-52)

## Central Argument

**Against the view that dialectics is "at most a rhetorical ploy"**:
- Marx's letter to Engels (1873): May have "overdone" dialectics in chapter on value with "modes of expression peculiar to him"
- **BUT**: "The whole book is woven with the thread of dialectics"
- Dialectics is not formal procedure but relates to entire content of *Capital*

## Main Dialectical Categories in Capital

### 1. Contradiction

**Definition**: "The idea that an entity involves within itself its opposite"

**Role**: "The red thread that connects the whole book from beginning to end"

**How it operates in Capital**:

#### Initial Form
- Commodity = unity of use-value and value

#### Successive Forms (Dualities)
The contradiction assumes different forms:
- **Concrete labour** ↔ **Abstract labour**
- **Commodity** ↔ **Money**
- **Production** ↔ **Circulation**
- **Labour process** ↔ **Valorisation process** (or "self-expansion of value")
- **Formal subsumption of labour** ↔ **Real subsumption of labour**

**Dynamic**: "It is the tension between and the interpenetration of these pairs that inevitably push the analysis forward at every stage"

### 2. Totality

**Source**: "Central to Hegel's dialectics"

**Role**: "Indispensable as a cornerstone of *Capital*"

**How it operates**:
- "Only on the basis of all three volumes that the pieces all fall together"
- Any treatment that neglects relations to the rest:
  - Impoverishes the analysis
  - Distorts the analysis
  - Or "even leaves devoid of meaning the part thus isolated from the whole"

**Implication**: Cannot understand any part of *Capital* in isolation from the whole

### 3. Essence and Appearance

**Nature of relationship**: Contradictory

**Importance**: "Vital to understanding the true nature of the capitalist mode of production"

**How it operates in Capital**:

#### True Relations (Essence)
- Value
- Surplus-value

#### Forms of Appearance (Phenomenal Forms)
- Price of production
- Profit

#### The Relationship
- "Seeming incongruity between the true relations and their forms of appearance"
- Phenomenal forms **conceal** essence
- This divergence **mystifies** capitalist relations
- Therefore: stands in need of **scientific demystification**

#### Volume III Focus
- "Replete with these phenomenal forms that conceal the essence"

#### Critical Point
**Phenomenal forms are NOT**:
- Figments of imagination
- Phantoms created by ideology

**Phenomenal forms ARE**:
- "The **necessary outward forms of appearance** of the inner forms"
- Reveal true nature of relations in:
  - Society of commodity producers
  - Mode of production based on wage labour

### 4. Mediation and Immediacy

**Source**: "So central to the Hegelian dialectic"

**Role**: "Vital to the analysis in *Capital*"

**Examples of application**:

#### Example 1: Crisis
"Without the operation of these concepts, one cannot for instance understand how crisis is at once:
- A disruption for capital accumulation, **but**
- At the same time the preparation of the conditions of another round of robust expansion of capital"

#### Example 2: Production Process
"Without the concept of **immediate unity**, one cannot comprehend how the process of production under capitalism is **at once**:
- A labour process, **and**
- A valorisation process"

### 5. Aufhebung

**Definition**: Simultaneous supersession and conservation

**Role**: "Already mentioned above as a cornerstone of dialectical development"

**Operation**: "Operative in its fullest sense in Marx's work"

**How it appears in Marx's works**:
- *Communist Manifesto*
- *Grundrisse*
- *Capital*

#### Application to Capitalism → Communism Transition

**What Marx elucidates**:

1. **Material forces built up by capitalism** prepare ground for communism

2. **"Civilising mission"** (*Grundrisse* term):
   - Capitalism undertakes this in its very process of development
   - Example: Creating unified world economy and politics

3. **Communism à la Marx**:
   - CANNOT be established except "on the ground already prepared by capitalism"
   - Abolition of capitalist private property
   - Establishment of new consciously ordained relations between producers
   - This will "certainly and irreversibly consign capitalism to the dustbin of history"
   - **BUT**: Material achievements of capitalism preserved
   - Civilising aspects of capitalism preserved under new mode of production

**The Transition as Aufhebung**:
> "Thus, the transition from capitalism to communism is in fact **simultaneously supersession and conservation**, in one single word *aufhebung*."

#### Why Aufhebung Matters
"Denying the importance of the dialectical method that Marx employs in laying bare the secret of capitalism and explaining its powerful tendency to create a communist society will inevitably lead, at a certain stage, to a renunciation of the major insights of Marx into capitalism."

## The Integrated Nature of Dialectics in Capital

### Not Merely Formal
"That this is not a merely formal procedure but relates to the whole content of *Capital* can only be made clear on the basis of an overall comprehension of the argument of the book."

### Requires Complete Understanding
To see how dialectics operates throughout *Capital*:

## Dialectics and the Subject Matter of Capital

### Capital as Book on Communism

**Key insight**: While *Capital* analyzes capitalism, its deeper purpose relates to communism

**How**:
- Shows how "in the womb of capitalist society, there unfolds a process of gestation of a different society"
- Different set of relations of production
- Based on:
  - Communal property (NOT private property)
  - Conscious planning (NOT market)
  - "Freely associated producers"

**Historical Movement**:
"The whole historical movement of capitalist society leads to the laying of the foundations of communist society"

**Marx's Unique Proposition**:
"Capitalism leads inevitably to communism unless some contingent historical factors hinder the transition"

**Contrast with Political Economy**:
- Political economy: capitalism conforms to human nature, therefore eternal
- Marx: capitalism is transient, prepares its own supersession

### Against Utopian Socialism

**What Marx does NOT do**:
- Does NOT draw blueprints for communist society
- Does NOT set up "lofty plans for the future society"

**Why not**:
- "For reasons we have already explained"
- Opposed to Utopian Socialists' method
- Marx carefully refrains from this approach

**What Marx DOES do**:
- Analyzes how capitalism itself creates conditions for communism
- Shows material preparation, not ideal design

## Implications for Understanding Capital

### 1. Dialectics is Structural, Not Decorative
- Not confined to chapter on value
- Woven throughout entire work
- Ignoring it = missing the point

### 2. Each Concept Has Role
- Contradiction: drives analysis forward
- Totality: requires all three volumes
- Essence/appearance: explains mystification
- Mediation/immediacy: explains complex processes
- Aufhebung: explains historical transition

### 3. Cannot Separate Method from Content
- Dialectical method IS the way Marx discovers capitalism's secrets
- Not added on after analysis complete
- Integral to investigation and presentation

### 4. Political Implications
- Understanding dialectics necessary for grasping communism's possibility
- Rejecting dialectics → rejecting Marx's major insights
- Particularly important for understanding transition

## Connection to Architecture of Capital

The dialectical method underlies the architectural structure:

1. **Contradiction**: Why move from one level to next (tensions push forward)
2. **Totality**: Why all four levels needed
3. **Essence/appearance**: Why Volume I (essence) and Volume III (appearance) both necessary
4. **Mediation**: How levels relate to each other
5. **Aufhebung**: How each level incorporates and transforms previous levels

## Summary: Why Dialectics Matters

**Theoretical level**:
- Only way to understand capitalism's contradictory nature
- Only way to grasp totality of three volumes
- Only way to comprehend essence behind appearances

**Political level**:
- Explains how capitalism prepares communism
- Shows transition as necessary, not utopian
- Reveals capitalism's historical limits

**Methodological level**:
- Provides rigorous method for moving from abstract to concrete
- Allows scientific demystification of capitalist relations
- Makes possible coherent analysis of complex, contradictory reality

## Key Theoretical Contributions

### Most Significant Insights

1. **Four-Level Architecture**
   - Provides organizing principle for all three volumes
   - Explains relationship between volumes
   - Prevents common misunderstandings

2. **Essential vs. Phenomenal Forms**
   - Not two different systems/realities
   - Phenomenal forms are *transformed* essential forms
   - Metamorphosis through *aufhebung*, not replacement

3. **Capital as Communism Book**
   - Shows how capitalism prepares its own supersession
   - Material forces + "civilizing mission" → communist foundations
   - Transition as *aufhebung*: supersession AND conservation

4. **Abstraction from Competition**
   - What makes "capital in general" (Vols I-II) possible
   - NOT assumption of identical organic compositions
   - Isolates capital-wage labour for "laboratory" study

### Methodological Innovations

1. **Investigation vs. Presentation**
   - Relations discovered empirically (investigation)
   - Presented logically/deductively (presentation)
   - Explains seeming "deduction from first principles"

2. **Rigorous Definition of Abstraction**
   - Study of determining forms/relationships
   - In isolation from incidental/secondary elements
   - Brings out innermost structure

3. **Historical Proof of Method**
   - Marx wrote Vol III (1864-65) before publishing Vol I (1867)
   - Knew about value-price divergence from start
   - Not a "difficulty" but capitalist reality itself

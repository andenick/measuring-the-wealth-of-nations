## Key Theoretical Contributions

### Most Important Concepts

1. **Four Levels of Abstraction** - Organizing principle for all three volumes of *Capital*
2. **Essential vs. Phenomenal Forms** - Volume I (values, surplus-value) vs. Volume III (prices of production, profit)
3. **Aufhebung in Transition** - How capitalism prepares and is superseded by communism
4. **Capital as Unified Social Science** - Not just economics but foundation for comprehensive analysis

### Critical Methodological Points

1. **Not Two Systems**: Volume III doesn't replace Volume I - phenomenal forms are modified essential forms
2. **Abstraction from Competition**: What makes "capital in general" possible (Vols I-II)
3. **Investigation vs. Presentation**: Relations discovered empirically, presented logically
4. **Marx Knew All Along**: Wrote Vol III before publishing Vol I - transformation not a "fix"

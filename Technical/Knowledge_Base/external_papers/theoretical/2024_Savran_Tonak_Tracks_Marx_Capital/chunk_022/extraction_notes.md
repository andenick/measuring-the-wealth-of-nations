### Case Study: Turkish Healthcare Workers

**Purpose**: Illustrate mediated impact of PUPL distinction

**Scenario 1 - MD with Private Cabinet**:
- Class position: Petty-bourgeois
- Exploits wage workers + self-exploitation
- Market competition forces closure → becomes worker in private hospital
- Class change: Petty-bourgeois → Worker
- Living standard reduction

**Scenario 2 - Nurse in Public Hospital**:
- Public employment: Unproductive worker (healthcare not commodity)
- Privatization → moves to private hospital
- Class position: Remains worker (no change)
- PUPL status change: Unproductive → Productive
- Material base shift: Wage funded by surplus-value produced elsewhere → now produces surplus-value herself
- **Key insight**: Surplus working time appropriated in BOTH cases

## Key Concepts and Terms

### Marxist Categories
1. **Productive Labour (PUPL)** - Labour that produces surplus-value for capital
2. **Unproductive Labour** - Labour that does not produce surplus-value but is exploited
3. **Rate of Surplus-Value (S/V)** - Identical to rate of exploitation for productive workers
4. **Rate of Exploitation** - Surplus working time / necessary working time
5. **Variable Capital (V)** - Capital spent on labor power
6. **Constant Capital (C)** - Capital spent on means of production
7. **Surplus-Value (S)** - Value appropriated from workers beyond necessary labor time

### Employment Categories
8. **Wage Workers** - Those employed for wages/salaries
9. **Self-Employed** - Petty commodity producers
10. **Petty-Bourgeois** - Small producers who exploit own labor and possibly hire workers

### Sectoral Classifications
11. **Productive Activities**: Agriculture, mining, manufacturing, utilities, construction, transport, hotels/restaurants, community services (excluding public admin)
12. **Unproductive Activities**: Trade (wholesale/retail), finance/insurance/real estate, public administration/defense

### Occupational Classifications
13. **Unproductive Professions**: Entrepreneurs, directors, senior management, administrative staff, commercial/sales staff
14. **Productive Professions**: Scientific/technical staff, service employees, agricultural workers

### Analytical Concepts
15. **Commodification** - Process of turning services into commodities
16. **Privatisation** - Transfer from public to private ownership
17. **Neoliberalism** - Economic restructuring phase emphasizing markets and privatization
18. **Class Position** - Location within relations of production
19. **Exploitation** - Appropriation of surplus labor time
20. **Rate of Exploitation** - Quantitative measure of degree of exploitation

### Methodological Terms

## Theoretical Contributions

1. **Extension of Exploitation Concept**
   - Rate of surplus-value applies to productive workers only
   - Rate of exploitation applies to ALL workers
   - Unproductive workers exploited but don't produce surplus-value
   - Their wages funded from surplus-value produced elsewhere

2. **PUPL Distinction Clarification**
   - Analytical tool for understanding capital dynamics
   - NOT a class criterion (contra Poulantzas)
   - Mediated relationship to class position
   - Healthcare workers example demonstrates this

3. **Empirical Marxism**
   - Shows labor theory of value CAN be measured
   - Transparent methodology despite data limitations
   - Addresses discontinuities in statistical systems

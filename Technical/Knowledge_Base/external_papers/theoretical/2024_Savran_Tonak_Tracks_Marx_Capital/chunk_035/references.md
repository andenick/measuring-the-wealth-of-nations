# References from Chunk 035 (Pages 321-330)

## Complete List of Citations

### A

1. **Amin, Samir (1977)**
   - Context: Cited as one who criticized and extended Emmanuel's theory of unequal exchange
   - Page: 321

2. **Amsden, Alice (1981)**
   - Context: Comparative analysis of rates of surplus-value in manufacturing industry (1969-1977)
   - Key contribution: International comparison showing South's rates exceeded North's
   - Quote: "An international comparison of rates of surplus-value throws light on the dimensions and possibilities of surplus-value creation under various historical and institutional conditions."
   - Pages: 326, 329-330

### B

3. **Bettelheim, Charles**
   - Context: Criticized and extended Emmanuel's theory of unequal exchange
   - Page: 321

### E

4. **Emmanuel, Arghiri**
   - Theory: Unequal exchange
   - Key argument: Capital is mobile and tends to equalize profit rates across the globe, while labor is relatively immobile
   - Challenged Sweezy's view on immobility of capital
   - Pages: 321

### G

5. **Gordon, David (1988, p. 59)**
   - Key finding: Low wages and surplus labour abroad have been much exaggerated as factors for FDI
   - Three most important factors for FDI: (1) proximity to large home markets, (2) relatively stable price and trade horizons, (3) general institutional climate and prospective evolution
   - Pages: 322-323

### H

6. **Higginbottom, Andy (2010)**
   - Context: Recent proponent of super-exploitation approach
   - Page: 330

### I

7. **Iguiñiz, Javier (1979)**
   - Doctoral dissertation at the New School (supervised by Shaikh)
   - Developed points also in Shaikh's work on unequal exchange
   - Context: Criticized and extended Emmanuel's theory
   - Pages: 321 (footnote 6)

### K

8. **Karahanoğulları (2009)**
   - Book: *Is Marx's Value Measurable?*
   - Period analyzed: 1988-2006
   - Finding: Average rate of surplus-value 239% (254% in 1988 to 312% in 2006)
   - Context: Most comprehensive analysis for Turkey
   - Methodology influenced by Shaikh and Tonak (1994)
   - Pages: 327-328 (footnote 8)

### M

9. **Mandel, Ernest**
   - Context: Criticized and extended Emmanuel's theory of unequal exchange
   - Page: 321

10. **Marini, Roy Mauro**
    - Original contribution to super-exploitation theory
    - Page: 330 (footnote 10)

11. **Mohun, Simon (2005)**
    - Updated Shaikh and Tonak's estimates with corrections based on newer data sets
    - Finding: Rate of surplus-value increased from 200% (1980s) to 300% (2000s)
    - Pages: 327-328 (footnote 8)

### P

12. **Paitaridis and Tsoulfidis (2012)**
    - Estimates for Greece using Shaikh-Tonak methodology
    - Page: 327 (footnote 8)

13. **Post, Charlie (2010, p. 21)**
    - Context: Discussion of labour aristocracy thesis
    - Referenced for methodology in Figure 3
    - Pages: 325, 326

### R

14. **Ricci (2019)**
    - Finding: Transfer of value outflow for the South ranged around 10-20%
    - Page: 321 (footnote 7)

15. **Roberts, M. (2019)**
    - Blog maintaining debate on super-exploitation
    - Page: 330

### S

16. **Satlıgan, Nail (2014)**
    - Context: Criticized and extended Emmanuel's theory of unequal exchange
    - Page: 321

17. **Shaikh, Anwar (1979, 1980)**
    - Seminal article on foreign trade, the law of value, and unequal exchange
    - Useful for identifying differences among theoreticians like Emmanuel, Mandel, and Amin
    - Pages: 321 (footnote 6)

18. **Shaikh, Anwar (2016)**
    - Quote: "the repression directed against labour beginning in the Reagan era had the clear effect of reversing the post-war pattern of profitability"
    - Page: 328

19. **Shaikh, Anwar and Tonak, E. Ahmet (1994)**
    - Method for estimating rate of surplus-value for US economy (1948-1989)
    - Finding: Rate increased from 170% (1948) to 244% (1989)
    - Provided methodological basis for estimates in many countries
    - Pages: 327 (footnote 8), 329 (Table 14.1)

20. **Smith, John (2016)**
    - Award-winning book on super-exploitation
    - Recent proponent of super-exploitation approach
    - Generated substantial debate
    - Page: 330

21. **Sweezy, Paul**
    - View: Equalization of rate of profit on world scale not possible due to immobility of capital
    - Challenged by Emmanuel
    - Page: 321

### T

22. **Tonak, E. Ahmet (1980)**
    - Early estimates for Turkey: 376% (1950) to 352% (1975)
    - Pages: 328 (footnote 9), 329 (Table 14.1)

23. **Tonak, E. Ahmet (1998)**
    - Explored profit-wage ratios in FDI-intensive enterprises in Turkey (1996)
    - Data source: Istanbul Chamber of Industrialists, leading 500 companies
    - Finding: Foreign joint ventures had 2-6 times higher profit-wage ratios than average
    - Page: 328

24. **Tricontinental (2019)**
    - Tricontinental Institute for Social Research
    - Empirical work showing firms in value chains in South imposed higher rates of surplus-value
    - Pages: 326, 330

25. **Tsoulfidis, Lefteris and Tsaliki, Persefoni (2014)**
    - Estimates for Greece using Shaikh-Tonak methodology
    - Finding: Rate of surplus-value changed from roughly 150% (1970) to 200% (2006)
    - Pages: 327 (footnote 8), 329

### U

26. **UNCTAD (2019)**
    - Source for FDI data and analysis
    - Quote about 2018 anomaly in FDI flows
    - Discussion of commodity capital vs. productive capital
    - Pages: 322

### V

27. **Venida (2007)**
    - Estimates for multiple countries in Table 14.1
    - Countries covered: USA, Philippines, Mexico, Puerto Rico
    - Page: 329 (Table 14.1)

### W

## Key Concepts/Terms Extracted

### Major Theoretical Concepts:
1. **Profit on Alienation (POA)** - Core concept of Chapter 8
2. **Surplus-Value** - Primary source of profit in capitalism
3. **Productive Labour** - Creates surplus-value
4. **Unproductive Labour** - Does not create surplus-value but is still exploited
5. **Industrial Profit** - Retained by productive capitalists
6. **Nonindustrial Profit** - Flows to trade and finance sectors
7. **Ground Rent** - From natural resources
8. **Profit of Enterprise** - Active entrepreneurial profit
9. **Interest** - Passive profit to lenders
10. **Transformation Problem** - POA helps explain

1. Neo-Ricardians (Sencer Divitçioğlu)

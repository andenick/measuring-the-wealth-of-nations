## Key Findings

### Chapter 12 Conclusions

1. **Digital economy is fully analyzable using Marx's labor theory of value**
   - Facebook produces commodities (targetable audiences)
   - Employs constant capital (infrastructure) and variable capital (labor)
   - Productive workers generate surplus-value
   - Distinction between productive/unproductive labor maintained

2. **"Immaterial labor" is actually material**
   - Digital products have electromagnetic existence
   - More tangible than many service industry outputs
   - Users are "petty commodity producers"
   - User content becomes means of production

3. **Universal applicability**
   - Analysis extends to all digital companies
   - No special exemption from value theory needed
   - Capitalism functions as expected in digital sector

### Chapter 13 Framework

1. **Purpose: Critical analysis of contemporary workplace practices**
   - Post-Fordism, lean production, flexible specialization
   - Total Quality Management, team work, flat organization
   - Claims these eliminate class conflict to be refuted

2. **Three objectives defined:**
   - Systematically explain new domination forms
   - Show Marxist categories remain adequate
   - Demonstrate these are part of capital's offensive since 1980s

3. **Core theoretical concept: Real subsumption of labor under capital**
   - **Formal subsumption:** Capital controls through social relations only
   - **Real subsumption:** Capital reshapes labor process itself
   - History of capitalism = deepening real subsumption
   - Lean production = highest stage of this process

4. **Political stakes:**
   - Counter bourgeois claims of class harmony
   - Address left's disorientation and demoralization
   - Show classical struggle methods remain relevant
   - Expose class character of "new" management techniques

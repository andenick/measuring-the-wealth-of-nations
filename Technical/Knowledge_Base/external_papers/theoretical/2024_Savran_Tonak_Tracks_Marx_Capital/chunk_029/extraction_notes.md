## 3. Content Summary

### Nature of Content
**Type:** Primarily **THEORETICAL** with some empirical reference

### Chapter 12 Summary (Conclusion)

Completes the analysis of Facebook as a case study demonstrating that digital economy companies:
- Produce commodities (targetable audiences)
- Extract surplus-value from productive workers
- Can be fully analyzed using Marx's labor theory of value
- Employ both constant capital (servers, buildings) and variable capital (labor power)
- Maintain distinction between productive and unproductive labor

**Key Argument:** Refutes claims that "immaterial labor" falls outside value theory. Digital products have material existence (electromagnetic) and are commodities like any other. Users function as "petty commodity producers" whose content becomes means of production for the platform's final product.

**Significance:** Establishes applicability of classical Marxist political economy to contemporary digital capitalism.

### Chapter 13 Summary (Introduction + Section 1 Beginning)

**Objectives:**
1. Systematically explain new forms of workplace domination (lean production, flexibility, etc.)
2. Demonstrate these can be understood using Marxist categories
3. Show these are part of capital's offensive since 1980s, not class harmony

**Critical Target:** Bourgeois theories claiming:
- Post-Fordism represents fundamental transformation
- New methods eliminate class contradictions
- Worker alienation and deskilling being overcome
- Marxist categories are obsolete

**Theoretical Foundation Established:**
- Production process as dialectical unity of valorization and labor process
- Formal vs. real subsumption of labor under capital
- Real subsumption = capital reshaping labor process to maximize surplus-value
- History of capitalism = deepening real subsumption

**Polemical Context:**
- Workers and left feel disoriented by new practices
- Fear that classical struggle methods are obsolete
- Chapter promises to show these fears are overstated
- But also that new methods are anti-worker, not harmonious

**Chapter's Position:**
- Consciously avoids detailed polemics to focus on systematic exposition
- Does not address "what is to be done" (reserved for future work)
- Acknowledges theoretical difficulty but insists on rigor (Marx: "no royal road to science")

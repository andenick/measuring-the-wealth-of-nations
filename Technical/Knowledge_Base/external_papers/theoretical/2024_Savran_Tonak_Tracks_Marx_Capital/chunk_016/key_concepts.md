# Key Concepts and Terms - Pages 151-160 (Book Pages 126-135)

## Major Theoretical Concepts

### 1. Historical Tendency of Capitalist Accumulation
- Central chapter discussed throughout this section
- Marx's synthesis of capital's development and inevitable contradictions
- Three-page chapter that presents "the path of development of modern history"
- Shows how capitalism creates conditions for its own demise

### 2. Dialectical Method
- Main methodological framework of Capital
- **Contradiction** as the main category
- Unity of opposites throughout Capital
- **Negation of the negation** - dialectical transformation

### 3. Economic Law of Motion
- The fundamental law governing capitalist development
- Based on "immanent laws of capitalistic production itself"
- Leads "incontrovertibly to the actualisation of communism"

### 4. Socialisation of Production and Labour
- Centralisation of production
- Formation of the "collective worker"
- Planning on large scale and international level
- Contradiction with private appropriation and the market

### 5. Capital as "Book of Communism"
- Central thesis of this chapter
- Capital explains not just capitalism but communism
- Shows capitalism creates historical conditions for communism

## Key Dialectical Pairs/Contradictions

1. **Use-value and Value**
   - Fundamental contradiction in the commodity
   - Basis for all subsequent contradictions

2. **Concrete Labour and Abstract Labour**
   - The "two-fold character of labour"
   - Marx's "best point" in Capital (according to himself)
   - "All understanding of the facts depends upon this"

3. **Production and Circulation**
   - Treated in Volumes One and Two respectively

4. **Labour Process and Valorisation Process**
   - Also called "process of self-expansion of value"

5. **Formal Subsumption and Real Subsumption**
   - Of labour to capital

6. **Socialised Production vs. Private Property**
   - The fundamental contradiction leading to capitalism's demise
   - "Planning within corporations coexists with anarchy"

## Historical and Social Concepts

### 7. Primitive Accumulation
- Process of separating direct producers from means of production
- Transformation of peasants, artisans into proletarians
- Prepares fundamental relationship of capitalist mode of production

### 8. The Proletariat
- "Universal class"
- Distinguished from all preceding classes by complete separation from means of production
- "Most adequate agency for the abolition of private property"
- Unlike serf, reaya, artisanal corporation members who had some possession

### 9. Expropriation of Expropriators
- "One capitalist always kills many"
- Centralisation of capital
- "The knell of capitalist private property sounds"

### 10. Collective Worker
- Result of cooperative form of labour process
- Integration of labour from isolation in small-scale units
- Product of large-scale, socialised production

## Structural Concepts Related to Capital

### 11. Three Volumes of Capital - Division of Labour

**Volume One:**
- Essence of production and reproduction of capital-wage labour relation
- Treated in isolation from intervening factors
- Appropriate location for historical law of motion

**Volume Two:**
- Circulation process
- Relationship between capital and wage labour

**Volume Three:**
- Transformation of essential forms into forms of appearance
- Division of surplus-value among capitals

### 12. Treatment of Surplus-Value
- Independent of particular forms (profit, interest, rent)
- Marx's second "best point" in Capital
- Unmasks exploitation

## Process Concepts

### 13. Centralisation of Capital
- "Centralisation of the means of production"
- Elimination of smaller capitalists
- Creates monopoly conditions

### 14. Internationalisation
- "Entanglement of all peoples in the net of the world-market"
- International character of capitalist regime
- Formation of world economy

### 15. Proletarianisation
- Ever-growing class of dispossessed
- Small-holder peasants, petty merchants, artisans yield place
- Weaker capitalists also become dispossessed

### 16. Revolutionising of Productive Forces
- Constant process under capitalism
- Cooperation, technical division of labour, factory production
- "Conscious technical application of science"

## Critique Concepts

### 17. Transformation Problem
- Obsession of "Marxian literature"
- Savran critiques focus on simultaneous equations
- Diverts from revolutionary content

### 18. Underconsumptionism
- Critique of Sweezy and Monthly Review school
- Distances from Marx's "immanent contradiction"

### 19. Commodity Fetishism
- Mentioned as commonly recognized concept from Capital

## Methodological Terms

### 20. Immanent Laws
- Laws internal to capitalist production itself
- Basis of historical necessity
- "Capitalism generates, within its bosom, the forces that will destroy it"

### 21. Necessity
- Marx "constantly talks of necessity"
- Development proceeds with "inexorability of a law of Nature"
- Not teleological but dialectical necessity

### 22. Contradiction Between Productive Forces and Relations of Production
- Fundamental Marxist concept
- Sweezy notably ignores this
- Central to understanding capitalism's limits

## Historical References

### 23. Reaya
- Subjects of the oriental ruler
- Had certain level of possession of land
- Contrasted with proletariat

### 24. Artisanal Corporation
- Pre-capitalist form
- Members had possession of means of production

### 25. Serf
- Pre-capitalist direct producer
- Had certain possession of land

### 26. Slave
- "Talking means of production"
- Different relation to means of production than proletariat

## Political/Revolutionary Concepts

### 27. Communism
- "Civilisation that is superior to itself"
- Will "submit to planning the productive forces"
- Eliminates private property, classes, and state
- "True history of humankind" begins
- Notably absent from David Harvey's Companion

### 28. Revolutionary Import
- Capital as revolutionary book
- Not just analysis but call to transformation
- Erased by technical "Marxian economists"

## Critical Assessment Terms

### 29. Lenin's Critique
- "Half a century later, none of the Marxists understood Marx!"
- Written in 1914 philosophical notebooks
- Based on deep study of Hegel's Logic

### 30. "Martians"
- Savran's term for "Marxian economists" with no affinity to Marx
- Those who erase revolutionary import
- "Communism is anathema to these dignified people"

## Author-Specific Concepts Critiqued

### 31. Harvey's "Accumulation by Dispossession"
- Harvey's own concept
- Diverts from Marx's actual argument
- "More of a guide onto himself than to Marx"

### 32. Reserve Army of Labour
- Mentioned as commonly recognized Capital concept

### 33. Tendential Fall of the Rate of Profit
- Mentioned as commonly recognized Capital concept

## Civilization/Historical Development

### 34. "Prehistory" vs. "True History"
- Communism takes society from prehistory to true history
- Fundamental transformation of human social existence

### 35. Technical Division of Labour
- In workplace and factory
- Renders production a collective process

### 36. Methodical Cultivation of the Soil
- Part of socialized labour development

### 37. Instruments of Labour Only Usable in Common
- Result of centralization and socialization

## Classical Political Economy

### 38. Classical Economy's Treatment
- Mixed particular forms with general form
- "Regular hash" according to Marx
- Marx's difference from classical political economy

## Meta-Theoretical Concepts

### 39. "Logic of Capital"
- Lenin: though Marx left no "Logic," he left logic of Capital
- Combines logic, dialectics, theory of knowledge of materialism
- "Three words are not needed: it is one and the same thing"

### 40. Red Thread
- Dialectical contradiction as connecting thread
- Runs from beginning to end of Capital

- Modern form of centralized capital

### 44. Corporations

- Large-scale planning units
- Planning within vs. anarchy between

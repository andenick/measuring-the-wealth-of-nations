## Key Findings and Concepts

### Major Empirical Findings

1. **Net Social Wage in Turkey (1980-2019)**:
   - Average NSW ratio: **-1.13% of GDP**
   - Predominantly negative throughout period (few exceptional years)
   - State redistribution favored capital over labour

2. **Labour Benefit Ratio**:
   - Average: **9.95% of GDP** (1980-2019)
   - Peaked at **60.44%** of labour income (2009)
   - Declined to **46.91%** of labour income (2019)

3. **Labour Tax Ratio**:
   - Average: **11.08% of GDP** (1980-2019)
   - Peaked at **56.82%** of labour income (2011)
   - Working population's share of total taxes: 35% (1980) → 55% (2019)

4. **Public Expenditure Allocation**:
   - ~30% of state revenues allocated to working people
   - Labour's share of expenditures: 23% (1980) → 44% (2019)
   - **But**: Population growth exceeded expenditure increases (per capita decline)
   - **And**: PPP guarantees artificially inflate labour benefit measurements

5. **Crisis Patterns**:
   - 1991, 1994, 1999, 2001 crises: NSW declined further negative
   - 2008-2009: Exception - NSW increased (expansionary fiscal policy)
   - 2017-2019: NSW varied between -0.05 and -0.51

6. **Rate of Surplus-Value Impact**:
   - Negative NSW increased effective surplus-value rate
   - State intervention favored capitalists over productive workers (1988-2006)

### Theoretical Contributions

1. **Pseudo-Welfare State Concept**: Applied to Turkey post-1980
2. **Class Bias of Fiscal Policy**: Demonstrated through NSW measurement
3. **Critique of AKP**: Demolished myth of pro-poor, pro-labour stance
4. **Tax System Analysis**: Shift from direct to indirect taxation burdens labour

**Location**: Page 359 (Page 340 in book)

- **Y-axis**: Percentage values ranging from 0 to -45

4. **Portugal (Por)**: -19.4%

5. **Middle performers**: US, France, UK, and Italy experienced moderate losses

### Context

This figure illustrates the overall slowing down of the economy internationally and at the level of imperialist capitalist economies since 2008, demonstrating the severity and unevenness of the crisis across different countries.

## Figure 2: Annual Rate of Growth of Net Capital Stock in Advanced Countries, 1981–2015

**Location**: Page 360 (Page 340 in book)

**Figure Number**: Fig. 2

**Full Caption**: "The annual rate of growth of the net capital stock in the advanced countries, 1981–2015 (Roberts, 2016, 241)"

### Visual Description

- **Type**: Line chart
- **Time period**: January 1981 to January 2015 (34 years)
- **X-axis**: Time (labeled at regular intervals: Jan-81, Jan-83, Jan-85, Jan-87, Jan-89, Jan-91, Jan-93, Jan-95, Jan-97, Jan-99, Jan-01, Jan-03, Jan-05, Jan-07, Jan-09, Jan-11, Jan-13, Jan-15)
- **Y-axis**: Percentage values ranging from 0.0 to 14.0
- **Line**: Single continuous black line showing fluctuating trend

### Key Patterns and Trends

1. **Initial decline (1981-1985)**: Sharp drop from approximately 13.5% to around 6.5%
2. **Recovery period (1985-1991)**: Fluctuation and partial recovery, with peaks around 8-10%
3. **Long decline begins (1991-2003)**: Steady downward trend from ~10% to ~3%
4. **Brief recovery (2003-2007)**: Moderate uptick to approximately 9%
5. **Crisis impact (2008-2009)**: Sharp decline
6. **Post-crisis stagnation (2009-2015)**: Continued decline to below 2%, reaching historic lows

### Critical Observations

1. **Overall trend**: Clear long-term decline in capital accumulation over the 34-year period
2. **Pre-2008 average**: Investment growth generally ranged between 6-10% before the crisis
3. **Post-2008 collapse**: Investment growth fell to approximately 1-2%, representing a nosedive
4. **No recovery**: Unlike previous downturns, there is no significant recovery after 2009
5. **Historic lows**: By 2015, the growth rate reached its lowest point in the entire period

### Context

This figure demonstrates that investment had already been on the decline for a long time before 2008, but it "took a nosedive in the period after 2008," illustrating the profound impact of the crisis on capital accumulation in advanced capitalist economies.

### Data Source

Roberts, 2016, p. 241

## Figure 3: Annual Growth Rate of Labour Productivity in G7 Countries, 1971–2020

**Location**: Page 361 (Page 341 in book)

**Figure Number**: Fig. 3

**Full Caption**: "The annual growth rate of labour productivity of the G7, 1971–2020 (GDP/labour hour) (Our calculations are based on data from the Organisation for Economic Cooperation Development)"

### Visual Description

- **Type**: Line chart with reference lines
- **Time period**: 1971 to 2020 (49 years)
- **X-axis**: Years from 1971 to 2019
- **Y-axis**: Percentage values ranging from 0 to 6%
- **Line**: Blue fluctuating line showing annual productivity growth
- **Reference lines**: Two horizontal dashed lines indicating average periods

### Key Data Points and Periods

1. **1971-1975**: High volatility, peak around 5% (1972)
2. **Average productivity growth 1971-2006: 2%** (marked with dashed line and annotation)
3. **1980s-1990s**: Generally fluctuating around 2-3%
4. **Late 1990s-early 2000s**: Some peaks around 3%
5. **2007-2020**: Sharp decline and stagnation
6. **Average productivity growth 2007-2020: 1%** (marked with dashed line and annotation)

### Critical Observations

1. **Pre-crisis baseline**: Average annual labour productivity growth was 2% between 1971-2006
2. **Post-crisis collapse**: Average fell to only 1% between 2007-2020
3. **50% decline**: The post-2007 average represents a 50% reduction compared to the previous 35-year average
4. **Volatility reduction**: Post-2007 period shows less volatility but consistently lower performance
5. **No recovery**: Unlike earlier downturns, productivity growth did not return to historical averages
6. **Dismal performance**: The text describes the post-2007 performance as "dismal" compared to the previous period

### Context

This figure illustrates the structural decline in labour productivity growth in the G7 countries during the post-2007 period, demonstrating that the crisis extended beyond finance and investment to affect fundamental productive capacity.

### Methodological Note

- **Measure**: GDP per labour hour
- **Data source**: Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD)
- **Calculation**: Authors' own calculations based on OECD data
- **Countries included**: G7 (Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, United Kingdom, United States)

### Data Source

Authors' calculations based on Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development data

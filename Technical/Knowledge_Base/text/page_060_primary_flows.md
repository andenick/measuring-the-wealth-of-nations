### 3.1 Primary flows: Production and trade

#### Key Definitions:

**Production:**
- Activities that create or transform material objects of social use (use values)
- Includes not only goods but also services:
  - Transportation
  - Entertainment
  - Lodging
  - Cooking
  - etc.
- Covers government enterprises that produce use values (e.g., electricity)
- Definition depends on character of process, not formal ownership

**Trade:**
- Wholesale and retail trade
- Rental of produced commodities (cars, buildings)
  - Viewed as piecemeal sale of product's use value over lifetime
- Government trading enterprises
- Transportation related to realization activities

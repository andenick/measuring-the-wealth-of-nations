# Page 80 - Government Activities in National Accounts

## Government Nonproduction Activities:
All of the following are classified as nonproduction activities:
- Police
- Fire departments
- Courts and prisons
- Defense and international affairs
- General administration

## Government Receipts:
1. Taxes (business and personal, net of subsidies and transfers)
2. Fees and fines
3. Rents and royalties
4. Personal and employer contributions for social insurance

## Government Disbursements:
1. Purchases of goods and services
   - Including payments to unproductive sectors
   - Including payments to government employees
2. Transfer payments to persons
3. Net interest paid to persons and foreigners
4. Net subsidies to government enterprises
   - Minus dividends received by government

## Input-Output Treatment:
### Revenue Side:
- Only picks up business payments to government
- Household payments (like royalties sector) don't show up explicitly

### Use Side:
- Transfer payments excluded (netted out of taxes and fees)
- Two major elements remain:
  - G': Government purchases of goods and services
  - W_G: Wages paid to government employees

## Key Inconsistency:
- G' treated as final use (implying nonproduction activity)
- W_G treated as intermediate input to "government industry"
- This creates theoretical inconsistency in the accounting framework

## Footnote:
¹⁵ Net interest paid to businesses is redefined as purchase of financial service in IO-NIPA accounts

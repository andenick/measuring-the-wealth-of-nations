# Page 110 - Figure 5.1: IO Accounts and Marxian Categories

## Figure Description:
Figure 5.1 provides a condensed matrix mapping between Input-Output accounts and Marxian categories, showing structural patterns of economic relationships.

## Matrix Structure:

### Rows (Economic Activities):
1. Production
2. Total Trade (private/public wholesale/retail + building/equipment rentals)
3. Royalties
4. Dummy Sectors:
   - Government Industry
   - Household Industry
   - Rest of World Industry
5. Gross Value Added
6. Gross Output

### Columns (Categories):
1. Production
2. Total Trade
3. Royalties
4. Dummy Sectors
5. Gross Final Demand
6. Gross Product

## Key Relationships:
- **Production, Trade, Royalties**: Each aggregated into single sectors
- **Value Added**: Shown gross of depreciation
- **Final Demand**: Shown gross of retirements
- **Intermediate Inputs**: Do not include fixed capital used up (D)

## Important Equation:
M'_p = (M_p - D_p)

Where D represents depreciation adjustment

## Special Elements:
- **Inventory Valuation Adjustment (IVA)**:
  - Merged into Gross Value Added (GVA)
  - Cross-hatching indicates inclusion in both GVA* and GFP*
  - Single entry shifted when merged

## Note:
Distributive transport was not estimated in this framework.

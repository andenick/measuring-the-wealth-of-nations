## Description

Figure 5.3 plots the Marxian rate of surplus value (also called the rate of exploitation), defined as e = S*/V*, over the period 1948-1989. This ratio measures how much surplus value is extracted per unit of variable capital — i.e., the share of the net product appropriated as surplus relative to the share going to productive workers' wages. The figure reveals the long-run trajectory of exploitation in the US economy, showing whether capital has succeeded in raising the rate of surplus value extraction over the postwar period. This is a foundational measure in Marxian economics, directly linking labor process outcomes to profitability dynamics.

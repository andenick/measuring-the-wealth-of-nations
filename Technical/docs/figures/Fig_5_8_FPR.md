## Description

Figure 5.8 provides a comprehensive cross-sectional comparison of Marxian and orthodox economic measures, drawing on the full range of Chapter 5 T-series (T501-T516). Based on Table 5.14, it juxtaposes key indicators — output, value added, profit rates, productivity, wages, and employment — as computed under both the Marxian and conventional national accounting frameworks. This summary figure crystallizes the central argument of Chapter 5: that the productive/unproductive distinction fundamentally alters the empirical picture of the US economy. Measures that appear stable or growing under orthodox accounting (e.g., profit share, productivity growth) may show stagnation or decline when recomputed using Marxian categories that exclude unproductive activities from the value product.

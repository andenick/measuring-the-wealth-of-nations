## Description

Figure 9.5 revisits the rate of surplus value (e = S*/V*) in the context of Chapter 9's analysis of accumulation and crisis. While Fig_5_3 introduced this measure in the accounting framework of Chapter 5, here in Chapter 9 the rate of surplus value is situated within the broader dynamics of capital accumulation: it is one of the key determinants (alongside the composition of capital and the share of reinvestment) of the rate of accumulation. The figure shows the long-run trend in the exploitation rate and its relationship to the declining rate of profit — in Marx's framework, a rising rate of surplus value can partially offset the effect of a rising organic composition of capital on the profit rate, but may not be sufficient to prevent the tendential fall. This figure provides the empirical evidence for evaluating that theoretical claim in the US postwar context.

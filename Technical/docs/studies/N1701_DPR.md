## Source Context

Applies ST framework to New Zealand. Key finding: post-1984 economic reforms increased the share of unproductive activity, with productive capital share declining from ~75% to ~65%.

## Subsources

- **N1701-A**: Synthetic series with structural break at 1984 reform. Quality: ESTIMATED.

## Transformation Chain

1. Generate pre-1984 series: gradual decline from ~75%
2. Generate post-1984 series: steeper decline from ~70% to ~65%
3. Add bounded noise, clip to [0.55, 0.80]

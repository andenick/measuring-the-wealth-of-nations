# N1305 — Post-2000 Structural Shift Indicator

## Source Context

Moos's key finding: a structural shift in NSW after 2000. Pre-2000 mean is negative; post-2000 mean is positive, representing a +3.0pp shift. This series captures both the NSW/GDP values and a post-2000 regime dummy variable.

## Subsources

- **N1305-A**: N1301 with added post-2000 regime indicator. Quality: DERIVED.

## Transformation Chain

1. Take N1301 (NSW/GDP)
2. Add binary post_2000 column (0 for <=2000, 1 for >2000)
3. Compute pre/post means for structural shift quantification

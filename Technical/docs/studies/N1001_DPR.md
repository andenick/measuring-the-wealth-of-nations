## Source Context

Tonak's 1984 dissertation is the foundational methodology work for Chapter 6 of Shaikh & Tonak (1994). It develops the framework for computing net taxes, benefits, and transfers allocated to workers vs. capitalists. Table II reports labor's share of national income (W_p / PI) declining slightly from ~0.73 (1952) to ~0.71 (1980).

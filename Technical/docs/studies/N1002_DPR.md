## Source Context

Tonak's dissertation finds that workers are consistently net subsidizers of the state — their tax payments exceed the benefits they receive. The net tax rate (T_labor - B_labor) / W_p is positive throughout the 1952-1980 period, rising from ~2% to ~4%.

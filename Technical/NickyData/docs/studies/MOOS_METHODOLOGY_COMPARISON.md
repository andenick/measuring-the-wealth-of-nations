# Moos (2017) vs AS2 NSW Methodology Comparison

## Summary
Our NSW computation (T607/P11) and Moos (2017) use the same Shaikh-Tonak framework but differ in detail. Moos Table 1 shows ~1% GDP difference for the overlap period (1959-1997).

## Key Differences

| Aspect | AS2 (P11) | Moos (2017) |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Benefits formula | B_w = transfers + govt services × worker share | E1 (direct) + E2 × LS (mixed) |
| Medicare/Medicaid | Included in transfers | Explicitly categorized as E1 |
| Veterans' benefits | Included | Excluded (cost of war, not social policy) |
| Tax allocation | Income-proportional (W_p/PI) | T1 (direct) + T2 × LS (mixed) |
| Labor share (LS) | Employee compensation / personal income | Same concept, potentially different NIPA lines |
| Defense exclusion | 40% of federal consumption | Not explicitly documented |

## Quantitative Impact
- Overlap period (1959-1997): Moos mean = 1.1% GDP, ST mean ≈ 0% GDP
- Difference: ~1.1 percentage points — attributed to NIPA revisions since ST's original data
- Both show same trend pattern (Moos Table 1, page 5)

## Conclusion
Differences are small (<1.5% GDP) and primarily due to data vintage differences, not methodology. Our approximation is adequate for the same qualitative conclusions.

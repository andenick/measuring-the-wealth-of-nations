# S514 - Capacity-Adjusted Profit Rate (r*_adj = r* x TCU/100)

**Status**: validated_book_and_extension (stock-form primary, v1.2 Iter 3)

**Chapter**: 5 supporting. **Units**: rate.

## Definition

Marxian profit rate adjusted for capacity utilization. Isolates underlying-trend r* from cyclical variation in capital stock usage. Per Shaikh & Tonak (1994), Chapter 5 p.124 and Table 5.11.

## Construction

Derived: r*_adj = S513 x (TCU / 100).

- **Primary form (stock)**: S514-A uses S513-A (stock-form book r*); S514-EXT uses S513-EXT (stock-form extended r*). Form cascades automatically from S513's form choice; see `S513_DPR.md`.
- **Secondary form (flow)**: S514-FLOW = S513-FLOW x TCU/100. Retained as reference variant only; NOT used in S514-COMBINED.
- TCU = Federal Reserve TCU series (cached at `data/raw/fred/fred_tcu_capacity_utilization.csv`).

## Coverage caveat

TCU is available 1967-present. Pre-1967 years (1948-1966, 19 years) emit explicit NaN with `provenance: TCU NaN pre-1967` per the no-synthetic-data rule. Coverage gap 1990-1997 inherited from S513-EXT (BEA NIPA component series).

## Anti-lazy-splice safeguard

# N1304: Moos vs ST Comparison 1959-1997 (Moos 2017)

**External Study** | study_04_moos_2017
**Period**: 1959–1997

## From the Book

> N1304 is the Moos (2017) NSW/GDP series restricted to the 1959-1997 overlap period with Shaikh & Tonak (2002). It is constructed by filtering N1301 to years 1959-1997. The purpose is to compare the two replications directly over the period where both have coverage, and to validate that the Moos methodology faithfully reconstructs the Shaikh-Tonak series (allowing for NIPA revision differences).

# N1302: NSW/EC Extended (Moos 2017)

**External Study** | study_04_moos_2017
**Period**: 1959–2012

## From the Book

> N1302 expresses Moos (2017)'s extended NSW series as a ratio to total employee compensation (EC), rather than GDP (N1301). This matches Shaikh & Tonak (2002)'s primary normalization (N1202). Moos primarily reports NSW/GDP (her Figure 1-2) but also presents NSW/EC in Figure 2 (1959-2012). The switch from GDP to EC as denominator amplifies the ratio since EC < GDP; the post-2001 peak is 16.1% of EC in 2010 vs 8.6% of GDP (from Table 2 summary statistics).

## How We Compute It

1. **derive**: `NSW / EC`

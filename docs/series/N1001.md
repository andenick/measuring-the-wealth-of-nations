# N1001: Labor Share of National Income (Tonak 1984)

**External Study** | study_01_tonak_1984
**Period**: 1952–1980

## From the Book

> Labor share (W/NI) is computed as the ratio of wages and salaries received by workers to national income. Tonak allocates NIPA employee compensation between labor and non-labor using an income-proportional 'labor share' factor (Total labor income / Personal income). For 1964 this factor is 0.73. The resulting series covers 1952-1980 and appears in Chapter IV, Table II ('Calculation of Labor Share, 1952-80'). The labor share is also used as the allocation key for Group II taxes and Group II expenditures throughout the dissertation.

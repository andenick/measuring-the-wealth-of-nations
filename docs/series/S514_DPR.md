# S514 - Capacity-Adjusted Profit Rate (r*' = r*/u, DIVIDE)

**Status**: validated_book_and_extension
<!-- status detail: stock-form primary; D1 2026-07-02 operation corrected multiply->divide -->

**Chapter**: 5 supporting. **Units**: rate.

## Definition

Marxian profit rate adjusted for capacity utilization, isolating underlying-trend r*
from cyclical variation in capital-stock usage. Shaikh & Tonak (1994) **Table 5.8**
defines it verbatim as **r*' = r*/u** (the profit rate is **DIVIDED** by capacity
utilization u). Book prose, Ch.5 p.124: "the mass of profit is divided by the rate of
capacity utilization, analogous to how actual output is divided by capacity utilization
to estimate potential output." **u is a fraction (0-1)**, u=1 = normal/full capacity.

> **D1 correction (2026-07-02):** the prior v1.2 implementation MULTIPLIED (r*_adj = r* x
> TCU/100), which ran below the book r*' and in the wrong conceptual direction (1989: old
> 0.31 vs book 0.39). It is now the book's DIVIDE. The old multiplied series survives as the
> clearly-labelled DEPRECATED comparison subseries **S514-AMULT** (two-arm rule). See
> `DIV-D02` in `DIVERGENCE_REGISTER.json` (patch: `internal-review-notes_2026-07/D1_DIV_PATCHES.json`).

- **S514-AMULT** (DEPRECATED): `S513-A x TCU/100`, the retired multiply operation. Do not use.
